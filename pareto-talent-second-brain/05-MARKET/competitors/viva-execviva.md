# Viva (ExecViva) — the most direct competitor on model

Full-time dedicated EAs from 10 countries in Central and South America (and South Africa), working US hours. Matched "usually within about 24 hours of the call." Aimed at **scaling tech companies and leadership teams** (logos/testimonials: Notion, Retool, Lovable, Clay, Veho, Owner), "100+ employees" per their Magic comparison.

## Pricing (billed quarterly)
| Plan | Price | Includes |
|---|---|---|
| Starter | $3,999/mo | 1 executive; CSM; 4+ weeks pre-training; free re-matches; <24 h onboarding |
| Enhanced (most popular) | $4,999/mo | up to 2 executives |
| Enterprise | $5,999/mo | up to 3 executives; PTO/leave backfill included |
"CEOs, owners, and founders count as 2 users" — so a founder starts at $4,999/mo.

## Claims
- Talent: undergraduate degree + 2+ years experience (5+ in comparisons); C1/C2 English; 60% have US work/study experience; "60%+ … hold active B1/B2 visas for US travel".
- "100% AI-fluent EAs trained on ChatGPT, Claude, Granola, and Zapier."
- "2+ year average EA tenure. 1 in 3 EAs stays 4+ years."
- "39% of customers save 2+ hours per day in their first month… 47% by Quarter 1"; "91% see value within the first 2 weeks"; "9+ hrs saved per executive, per week".
- Guarantee: free re-matches; 24-hour replacement.

## Marketing tactic: comparison pages
https://execviva.com/resources/comparisons/ — "Viva vs" pages for Athena, BELAY, Boldly, Magic and in-house hiring. Bottom-of-funnel SEO for buyers already comparing. CTA "Book a Call"; no email capture or gated lead magnet. Their claims about rivals (third-party, unverified):
- **Athena:** Philippines and Kenya talent, 11–14 h offset ("graveyard shifts"), 8–12 weeks / 2–3 weeks to start (both stated), "high turnover in reviews", 12-month commitment, from $3,000/mo; best for solopreneurs and first VA hires.
- **BELAY:** US 1099 contractors, fractional 10–20 h/week, ~$42/hr (~$1,380 for 30 h/mo) while paying VAs $19–25/hr; best for churches, law firms, consultants.
- **Magic:** Philippines, pooled or junior dedicated, ~$13.50/hr; solopreneurs and small businesses under 20 FTEs.
- **Boldly:** fractional model.
- **In-house:** you handle recruiting, training, covering absences.
