**Where Pareto wins:** the only one that trains the founder too (weekly live mastermind), the strongest guarantee set (Lifetime Replacement + Freedom 40), you choose from 3 candidates in 24 hours, AI-trained operators, founder-led SMB focus (Viva targets tech teams, Athena first-time VA buyers).
**Proof gap to use:** CloudTask's headline retention is 85% at 90 days; Pareto's is 93% at 12 months.
**Where Pareto is exposed:** Rose is $2,500/mo flat and Athena $3,000/mo on annual terms; HireLATAM is a one-time fee; Viva publishes tenure data (2+ yr average) and big-tech logos.
Competitor claims about each other come from Viva's comparison pages and are unverified.

**Tactic worth copying:** Viva's "Viva vs X" comparison pages catch buyers already shopping. Pareto has none.
