Our lead magnet should fill a gap, not duplicate these.

| Asset | Format | Price | Promise | Capture / qualification | After opt-in |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **HIRE** book ([[thehirebook]], content: [[hire-book]]) | Book PDF + resources (also Amazon $9.99) | Free | "Learn how to FIND & MANAGE the team that will make you MILLIONS" | Long form: name, email, phone, company size, revenue, hiring timeline, pain points | Thank-you page delivers instantly, "check your email" (Day 8) |
| **The Second Brain for Founders** guide ([[paretotalent-guide-second-brain-for-founders]]) | Ungated web guide | Free | Get context out of your head so your team and AI can act for you | None (CTA: Book Matching Call ×3; workshop waitlist) | — |

| **Bootcamp** | 10-day live training | Free | For talent, not founders | Name + email first, then application | Nurture to applicants who drop off |

**Signal for our project:** a second brain is the "number one requested thing by entrepreneurs" (Ivan, Day 3), and Pareto's best-performing page is the Second Brain Workshop (Day 6). But Pareto already owns a second brain guide and workshop, so a new magnet should either go deeper on a different pain (delegation readiness, inbox, "what to hand off first", cost of doing it yourself) or clearly complement it. ## What the HIRE book itself sells ([[hire-book]], full read)
No Right Hand Program, no placement service, no pricing or guarantee anywhere in the book. Its only CTAs: free templates at TheHireBook.com/Free (job posting, trial project offer and details, rejection letter, schedule email, contractor agreement, global job board, cheat sheet, workbook), a free upcoming delegation book via the newsletter, a review request, and a free 5-day training for readers who tag @KasimAslam.
**Gap this leaves:** the book teaches founders to do all 7 steps themselves (thousands of applications, two filter rounds, paid trials, interviews, 90-day onboarding) and even says "Your EA should" do the screening. Nothing in it bridges to "or let Pareto do it for you". A lead magnet can own that bridge.
