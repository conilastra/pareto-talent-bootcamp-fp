# Skills, connectors and automation

Skill = one capability, one file (name & description, tools, instructions, standard); narrow, with a quality bar "you could argue with". Every automation = trigger → condition → action → delay. "An API is the phone line, a webhook is the phone ringing." "Connections are permissions": scope down. "Draft, don't send" checkpoint; 80% ship rule. A Right Hand's four week-one builds: lead dossier, department status summary, meeting recap, daily morning brief.
