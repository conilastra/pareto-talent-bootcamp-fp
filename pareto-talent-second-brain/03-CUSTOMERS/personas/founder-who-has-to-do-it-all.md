# The founder who believes they have to do it all

## Cause of overwhelm
Delegating feels like failing, so every decision, email and follow-up runs through the founder. The most common story in the research (about 40 BELAY stories, plus DonnaPro, South and HireLATAM).

## What they say (verbatim, with source)
- "We all think as business owners that we can just do it all." (BELAY client)
- "We work in the business too much and not on the business." (BELAY client)
- "I did not want to admit that I needed help." (BELAY client)
- "I'll just work more." (BELAY coach)
- "My mindset before was that I need to do it all... it was slowing me down and costing my business money." (DonnaPro client, Pawel Nical)

## Situation and trigger (why now)
A cost shows up: work spills into family time, or "we had to turn down new projects because I was completely bogged down by administrative bottlenecks." (DonnaPro client)

## What they've tried
Working more.

## Objections
Letting go of control; admitting they need help; handing over inbox and calendar.

## What "freedom" means to them
Headspace to work on the business, not in it; time with family.

## Best lead magnet angle
Show the hours and the cost of doing it all themselves, with the number first.

## Best ad angles
1. "You didn't start a company to be its inbox."
2. "What would you do with 10 more hours a week?"

## Qualification signals
Team of 2 or more, founder still handling email and calendar, wants relief now.
