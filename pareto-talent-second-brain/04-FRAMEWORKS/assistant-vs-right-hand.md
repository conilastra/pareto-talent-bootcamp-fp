# Assistant vs Right Hand

| Assistant | Right Hand |
|---|---|
| Waits | Notices what's missing |
| Stops at task end | Closes the loop unchased |
| "You didn't tell me to check that" | Has already drafted the fix |
| Works from a job description | Works from the founder's outcome |
| "I don't know that tool" | Learns the tool, then teaches the founder |

Six areas a Right Hand owns (~3 months): inbox & calendar; second brain; projects & follow-through; systems & automation; content & comms in the founder's voice; client-facing work.
Pareto says "executive assistant" or "Right Hand", never "virtual assistant".
