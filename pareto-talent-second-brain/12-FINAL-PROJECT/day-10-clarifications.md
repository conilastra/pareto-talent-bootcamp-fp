# Day 10 clarifications (what Ivan and the CSM team said that the brief does not)

Spoken answers from [[bootcamp-day-10]]. Where they differ from the written brief ([[final-project-guidelines-full-text]]), the written brief and its slides still rule unless noted. Confirm with the Pareto team if unsure.

## Resolves a conflict
- **Number of ads**: five **images**, not three. Ivan: it was one video + three images; "I removed the video and I left five images instead". Some slides still say 3. Best format: **4x5** (Facebook feed). A video is optional ("as a proactive step"). → closes [[open-questions]] #17.
- **Emails**: qualified follow-up = **3 emails**; unqualified = **1 email** that delivers the lead magnet. → closes [[open-questions]] #11.

## Audience and offer
- The landing page speaks to **entrepreneurs/founders**, not to prospective Right Hands.
- Founders are mostly **founder-led companies**; about 5 of 100 clients are enterprise (C-suite). A niche (e.g. **lawyers**, "a good niche for us") is allowed.
- **Continuity** from lead magnet to the call: the magnet reveals a problem or score (e.g. delegation score 50/100, or an inbox tool), then the offer is "do you want somebody to do it for you? Speak with Pareto, they will give you a Right Hand."
- Lead magnet can be anything: PDF, checklist, quiz, calculator, mini app, Claude skill, agent. Must qualify the person. A weak lead magnet choice costs you at most points in the lead-magnet category, not the whole project.
- Brand: any Pareto resource may be used. Palette may differ if you argue it converts better (the HIRE book funnel is white with blue). The site uses both blue and green; see [[visual-identity]].
- Do the brief first, then add recommendations as "extra mile"; do not replace what was asked.

## Build rules
- Build the GHL workflows but leave them **off** (avoid loops that send "a million emails"). Test the chain yourself; GHL workflow logs show whether a trigger fired even if the email lands elsewhere.
- Ads are copy + image only; do **not** run or spend on Facebook.
- Loom: 2 minutes, a quick run-through of what you built and the logic ("compress a 45-minute presentation"); **camera on recommended**.
- PDF: no page-count rule; be concise and structured. Link index labels are in the guidelines doc (L1 second brain, L2 research doc, L3 business map, L4 ClickUp board, L5 SOP, then L6 onward for lead magnet, landing page, forms and so on).
- Test every link in an incognito window before submitting.

## Scoring and process
- 20/25 minimum, but it can be uneven (Ivan's example: 5, 5, 4, 4, 2 passes).
- At 18-19 Ivan also checks homework and attendance. Final project is the priority over homework; certificate needs at least 15.
- Early submission is possible; he scores as they come in. Resubmission is "primarily one shot", but if scored early there may be time to resubmit (no feedback given).
- Nobody scores 100%: prioritising is part of the test.
- Homework from other days can still be re-sent.
- Interview: normal conversation, can reject on communication or culture fit even with a passing project.
