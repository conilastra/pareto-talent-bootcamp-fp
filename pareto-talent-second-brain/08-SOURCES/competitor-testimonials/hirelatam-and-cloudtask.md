# Patterns (counts are across the 13 captured items: H1 to H9 and C1 to C4)

Overall: HireLATAM's proof is aimed at SMB owners and founders (plumbing, real estate, a solo speaker, founders on social media). CloudTask's proof is entirely enterprise sales/CS scale-ups. Only **1 item** (H6, Rob LaBonne) is an executive assistant hire, and **1** is a marketing assistant (H2).

**Top pain points / BEFORE**
- Local payroll too high, or a tight local labour market: 3 (H1, H3, H7 implicitly)
- Internal team can't keep up with growth or demand: 3 (C1, C2, C3)
- Hard to find reliable, skilled talent, or too many unqualified applicants: 2 (H3, plus the HireLATAM positioning line; H2 implicitly)
- Sudden departure / urgent backfill: 1 (H7)
- Founder stuck in day-to-day operations: 1 (H8)

**Triggers**
- Growth spurt, IPO or acquisition mandate: 3 (C1, C2, C3)
- Peer referral: 1 (H1)
- Seeing the team in person (Medellín visit): 2 (C1/Tim Zheng, C4)
- Employee loss: 1 (H7)
- Wish to step back and be "more strategically involved": 1 (H8)

**Expectations**
- Speed of hire: 5 (H2, H7, C1, C2, C3)
- U.S. time-zone overlap: 4 (H1, H3, H6, C4)
- Vetted quality / curated shortlist, not volume: 4 (H2, H4, H5, H9)
- Keeping control of quality at scale: 2 (C2, C3)

**Value drivers (AFTER)**
- Talent quality ("rock star", "A Player", "first-rate", "legit contributors from week one"): 7 (H2, H4, H5, H6, H7, H9, C2)
- Cost savings vs. local hiring: 4 (H1 at 60 to 70%, H3, H7, plus the $3,500 flat fee offer)
- Scale / headcount growth: 5 (H1, H4, C1, C2, C3)
- Reliability / loyalty / retention: 4 (H1 "loyal", H3 "reliable", H6 "super reliable", C2 "5+ years")
- Culture fit / work ethic: 4 (H6, C2, C4, H1)
- English fluency: 1 (H6). Personal productivity: 1 (H6)
- Revenue / business outcome: 3 (C1, C2, C3)

**Objections / hesitations stated:** almost none on these pages. Implied: SF-only bias before seeing LATAM (C1), worry about losing quality control at scale (C3), and the pain of an urgent backfill (H7). The videos and gated PDFs may hold more (not accessed).
