# Four guarantee types

1. **Unconditional** — money back no matter what
2. **Outcome-based** — "We get you to X, or we keep working for free until we do"
3. **Conditional** — do the work, get the guarantee (Pareto's Freedom 40 is this type)
4. **Anti-guarantee** — all sales final; signals exclusivity
