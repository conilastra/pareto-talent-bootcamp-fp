Final Project Guidelines · The Pareto Talent Lead Magnet

Use these guidelines to present your final project. Every submission is checked by AI first, then reviewed by the Pareto team. Follow this structure exactly.

Deadline: end of day, October 7th. No extensions. Submit at: bootcamp.paretotalent.com/finalproject What you submit: ONE PDF.

1. The project in one paragraph

Pareto Talent needs more qualified founders booking calls. You build a lead magnet founders actually want, and the funnel around it: a landing page that sells it, a form that qualifies whoever requests it, and two different next steps. Qualified founders are redirected to book a call with Pareto. Everyone else lands on a thank you page, gets the resource, and enters a nurture sequence. Every part of the project should lead a founder to one conclusion: I need a Right Hand, and Pareto has one for me.

2. How your submission is checked

Before anything is scored, an automated check runs on your PDF:

It reads your Link Index (Section 2) and finds every label, L1 to L15 and H1 to H9.

It opens every single link.

It confirms each link loads, is public, and matches what its label says it is.

It checks that every Part heading exists in your walkthrough (Section 3).

Any link that doesn't open disqualifies the whole project. Nothing gets scored until every link passes.

3. Automatic disqualifiers

A link that doesn't open. Errors, private files, or broken pages.

Login walls. If we need an account or have to request access, the link is broken. Set everything to &quot;Anyone with the link can view.&quot;

Shortened links. No bit.ly, no link-in-bio pages. Every URL written out in full, starting with https://.

Late submission. After end of day, October 7th.

4. The PDF rules

One PDF, three sections, in this order: Cover, Link Index, Project Walkthrough.

File name: FP_FirstnameLastname_ParetoBootcamp.pdf (for example, FP_AnaGomez_ParetoBootcamp.pdf)

Write it in Google Docs and export to PDF. Selectable text makes your project much easier to review.

Every URL written out in full, not hidden behind &quot;click here.&quot;

Didn't build something? Write its label followed by a dash, for example L3 · -. Never delete the line.

Don't share your project in Slack or anywhere public.

SECTION 3 · PROJECT WALKTHROUGH

For every Part, write three things: what you built, the key decisions and why, and which index links it refers to. Screenshots are welcome, but every claim must also be written in text.

Which sources you fed it, and which Pareto frameworks it holds.

Pareto's ICP in one paragraph, and your 3+ founder personas, by cause, in client words.

The journey you mapped, from ad to call, and any gaps you marked honestly.

Part 3 · ClickUp breakdown and SOP (L4, L5)

Part 4 · The lead magnet and the offer (written here, no link needed)

The title (MAGIC formula) and how it pushes the Value Equation.

Your qualification criteria: exactly who counts as qualified, and why.

The next-step offer: why a qualified founder should book a call about the Right Hand Program.

Part 5 · The lead magnet itself (L6)

What's inside it, and how you built it with your Second Brain.

How it's delivered automatically by email.

Part 6 · The funnel (L7, L8, L9, L10)

Which tool you used, and why.

The landing page sections, in order.

Your qualification questions, and how the form redirects qualified and unqualified leads.

What the booking page and the thank you page each include.

Part 7 · CRM and automation (L11, L12)

Your pipeline stages, including where unqualified leads go.

Each workflow: trigger, actions, and email timing.

Opt-in workflow (deliver, tag, opportunity, notify yourself)

Qualified follow-up (2 emails to anyone who qualified but didn't book)

Unqualified nurture (3 emails)

Booking workflow (confirmation and reminder)

How you tested the full chain.

Part 8 · Launch materials (L13, L14)

The 5 ads, and the persona and angle behind each. Five different messages, not five hooks on one.

The prompts you used for the imagery.

Part 9 · Loom (L15)

One line on what the Loom covers. Keep it to 2 minutes.

What else would you bring?

Anything extra you added that wasn't asked for, and why.
