# Job post anatomy (job post = sales letter)

"Your job posting is a sales letter—it should be written with your marketing hat on, not your HR hat."

11 parts: Title · Heading · Who we are · What we're looking for · Job description · Salary & growth · Personality profile · Make sure you're comfortable with (skunks) · Values · To apply · Close.

- **Title formula:** "WANTED! The World's [Adjective] Remote [Position]".
- No utility players. "The one exception is Executive Assistants. Their job is the ambiguity."
- Responsibilities written from the end output (~5, specific); qualifications broad.
- Salary exact: "starts at $X per [interval]", never a range, plus an ascension path.
- Values: hire, manage and fire by them (Kasim: Truth, Responsibility, Heart/Love).
