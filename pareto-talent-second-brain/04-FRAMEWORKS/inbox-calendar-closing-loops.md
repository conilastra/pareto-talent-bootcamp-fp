# Inbox, calendar and closing loops

"You're not hired to answer email, you're hired to close loops." Inbox Zero = zero open loops, not zero emails; archive older than an agreed cut-off (30 → 60 → 90 days); a rule book per founder; inbox access in 3 stages (forwarding → delegated drafts → replying to ~90%); Shortwave recommended. A half-right draft saves the founder ~80% of the time. Calendar: time blocking, protected deep work, ghost events, color-coding, buffers, Friday planning. These are concrete founder outcomes a lead magnet can promise.
