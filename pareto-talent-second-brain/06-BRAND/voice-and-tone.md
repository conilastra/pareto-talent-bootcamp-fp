# Voice and tone

**Three words:** direct, plain-spoken, provocative (in a confident, slightly contrarian way).

## Rules
- Short sentences. Lead with the answer. Kasim's writing: "short sentences, very, like, direct."
- Use client words, not marketer words ([[client-language]]).
- Numbers beat adjectives ("A number beats an adjective").
- Reframe the problem: "You don't have a people problem. You have a delegation problem."
- Contrast pairs: "Not a placement. A complete delegation system." "Not a gig. A serious job." "Terms we put in writing, not promises."
- Aim negation at the reader, not a trend.
- AI-forward, but the person is the point: AI is "a multiplier, not a replacement."
- Honest scarcity only.

## Say / never say
| Say | Never say |
|---|---|
| Right Hand, executive assistant, operator | virtual assistant, VA (except to describe what failed) |
| delegation, founder freedom, close the loop | "outsourcing", "cheap labor" |
| founders, entrepreneurs | "users" |
Never invent a statistic. Never use em dashes (Day 2 instruction example). Never apologise. No "AI slop".
