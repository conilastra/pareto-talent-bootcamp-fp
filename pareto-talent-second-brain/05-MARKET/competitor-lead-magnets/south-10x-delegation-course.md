# South — 10X Delegation Course

## Format
Free email course, delivered in installments. Length not stated. South's resources are all email-gated (confirmed by Conil, 2026-10-03).
## Promise
"Learn to Delegate and 10x Your Productivity": delegation strategies, documentation methods, common pitfalls, with 50+ example tasks by function (operations, marketing, finance, sales).
## Who it's for
Anyone who wants to delegate; not limited to founders.
## How they capture
Email signup behind the "Get the Free Course" button.
## How they qualify
Not visible. Qualification, if any, happens on the sales call.
## What happens right after opt-in
Email 1 arrives from the founder, Colin Keeley (personal tone, built via Beehiiv). Captured 2026-10-03, pasted by Conil. Structure:
- **Disclosure line:** "Why you're getting this", then the pitch: "save 70% on employee costs by hiring the top 1% Latin American talent. Interview for free!"
- **Hook, "The Art of Leverage":** same 24 hours; leverage = more with the same effort; delegation is the easiest route. "Hire a full-time assistant" = 16 working hours, then 24, then 36.
- **Roadmap:** what to delegate (50+ ideas), how to delegate well, finding people to delegate to (next emails).
- **Method:** track your day/week in a spreadsheet; 3 columns (Do I like it? Is it repeatable?); no/yes rows are the first to delegate. Then "what would you do with the extra hours?"
- **The 50 ideas** in five groups: Operations/Customer Service (12), Sales (9), Marketing Paid (6), SEO/Content (5), Marketing Other (9), Finance (4). Task-level and mostly junior/VA work (data entry, PDFs, transcription, CRM clean-up, ad launches, bookkeeping).
- **Engagement CTA:** "what would you say your biggest struggle is right now when it comes to delegation?" Reply invited ("I read every response!").
- **Sales CTA:** "Start hiring" button at the bottom; footer is unsubscribe plus Austin, TX address.
Tone: conversational, short sentences, no branding beyond text.
## Strengths
The only competitor magnet in Pareto's territory (delegation). Multi-day email format builds a relationship and keeps South in the inbox.
## Weaknesses
Generic task list for any role or company size; no personal result; no qualification. The tasks are low-level (data entry, PDFs, bookkeeping), so it attracts VA-type buyers, not founders after a strategic Right Hand. Delegation is the hook but the sell is cost ("70% savings"), and the "Start hiring" button is a single CTA with no routing. One real strength: the closing question invites a reply and surfaces the reader's biggest delegation struggle, which is raw voice-of-customer.
## What Pareto can do better
Own delegation as authority, not a task list. Borrow what works (the personal founder voice, the track-your-tasks method, the closing reply question), but add what South lacks: personalised result, 2-3 qualifying questions, qualified founders routed to a call about the Right Hand Program.
