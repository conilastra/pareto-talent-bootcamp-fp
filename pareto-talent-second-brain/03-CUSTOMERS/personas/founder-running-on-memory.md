# Founder running the business from memory (candidate)

## Cause
50% of founders run everything from memory; 40% let things fall through the cracks. "The average entrepreneur keeps 14 different systems in mind."

## What they say
- "A lot of things for entrepreneurs runs from their memory." (Ivan)
- "things fall through the cracks… chase invoices… opportunities, referral emails… they start missing things." (Ivan)
- "you will not have the full context, you will not know how to reply" (founder objection, Day 3)

## Lead magnet angle
Second brain / context capture. Note: Pareto already has an ungated guide and a paid workshop on this ([[existing-lead-magnets-and-front-end-offers]]).

## Ad angle
"Don't let work slip through the cracks." (Day 6 emotional angle)
