# G.R.O.W.T.H. core values, expectations and dealbreakers

Source: Day 10 slides and Ivan's talk ([[bootcamp-day-10]]). These describe how Pareto treats its **Right Hands** (talent side). Use them to understand who the Right Hand is, not as founder-facing copy without checking.

## Values (every Right Hand reads and signs them in the contractor agreement)
| | Value | Slide wording |
|---|---|---|
| G | Growth Through Feedback | Open feedback, given and received as a path to growth, not taken personally |
| R | Respect Boundaries | Communicate boundaries, ask for help, work-life balance, "not suffering in silence" |
| O | Own and Lead Boldly | Don't wait for direction; initiative, extra mile, proactive communication |
| W | Win and Have Fun Together | Celebrate each other's wins (Slack #wins channel); professional but relaxed |
| T | Teach and Grow | Share best practices, teach each other, continuous learning |
| H | Honesty First | Address mistakes openly and fix them fast ("radical honesty", Ivan) |

## Three expectations
1. **Communication**: say where things stand before anyone asks; flag problems early.
2. **Proactivity**: spot what is missing and move; bring answers, or options with the question.
3. **Technical self-reliance**: Google, then Claude, then YouTube, then the community and the architects.

## Dealbreakers
Lack of initiative · dishonesty · inconsistency · breaking confidentiality. See [[bootcamp-day-10]] for Ivan's reasoning (including "AI is a task-taker").

## What the Right Hand gets
Starting salary $1,000+/month, 20 days PTO, USD via Wise or Payoneer, tools and equipment support, weekly trainings, 1:1 coaching, lifetime replacement, no spyware. Full list in [[bootcamp-day-10]]. Related: [[right-hand-program]] · [[assistant-vs-right-hand]] · [[team]]
