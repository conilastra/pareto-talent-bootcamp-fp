## Implications for Pareto Talent (from this VOC only)
- Both competitors lead with time back and peace of mind. BELAY's proof rests on matching, vetting and trust. Viva's rests on tech-company logos and "no career-management overhead."
- The most common trigger is "previous help failed." This includes a named failed offshore VA (B104) and time-zone and cost barriers (B77). Pareto should address quality and time-zone fit directly.
- BELAY's US-based talent is a selling point for confidentiality and time zones. Pareto will meet these objections: remote/offshore skepticism, confidentiality, voice, and "can they work as if internal."
- BELAY loses about 12 contractors to clients who convert them to full-time. A placement model that expects this, with conversion paths or a placement fee, may appeal to buyers who end up wanting to own the hire.
