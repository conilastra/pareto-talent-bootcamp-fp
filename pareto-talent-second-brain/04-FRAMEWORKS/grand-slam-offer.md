# Grand Slam Offer (5 parts)

1. **Audience** — exact person, stage, role, normal week
2. **Problem** — the single pain "keeping them up at night, in their own words"
3. **Solution** — the outcome, not the tool
4. **Bonuses** — knock out the next objection
5. **Guarantee** — remove risk from their side

Build it with an inverse interview: "ask me 20 questions you'd need to build me a perfect offer." "The offer comes before the page." Related: [[value-stacking]], [[guarantee-types]], [[value-equation]].
