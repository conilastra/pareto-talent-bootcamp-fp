# CloudTask

"Hire an executive assistant who protects your calendar and your focus." Dedicated (not pooled) virtual EAs from Latin America and the Caribbean (12 countries, e.g. Colombia, Argentina, Jamaica). For founders, executives and teams.

## Models and price
- **Managed Staffing:** "One all-in monthly rate" (quoted per role, not public); CloudTask handles payroll and compliance.
- **Direct Hire:** one-time fee of "20% to 30% of first-year base salary".
- **"$299 to start your search. One time, not a subscription."**

## Process
Post the role (5 minutes) → 3–5 matched profiles within 48 hours → interview and hire → onboarding check-ins at days 0, 7, 30, 60.
Role brief asks: what scope you'd hand over, what tools you use, what hours need coverage, how much executive support you need.

## Vetting and guarantee
"Human + AI verified"; live-call screening for judgment, escalation and discretion; "Tool Verification: Verified per candidate" (Google Workspace, Outlook, Slack, Notion, HubSpot, Salesforce). **6-month replacement guarantee** (uncapped replacements on Managed Staffing; one on Direct Hire).

## Proof
10,000+ hires since 2016; "85% still in seat past 90 days"; Clutch 5.0, Trustpilot 4.6, G2 4.9; client tenure "5 to 8 years" claims. Testimonials: David Barrett (CEO, Expensify), Tim Zheng (CEO, Apollo.io), Carl Carell (CRO, GetAccept).

## Lead magnet
Ungated hiring guide embedded on the page, "How to hire an executive assistant" (role definition, screening, red flags, cost). No email capture. CTAs: "Get Matched", "Start your search", "Book a meeting" (HubSpot).

## Pareto vs CloudTask
Same region and similar speed (3–5 profiles in 48 h vs Pareto's 3 in 24 h). CloudTask competes on volume and a $299 entry fee; its proof metric is 85% at 90 days, while Pareto's is **93% at 12 months**. Pareto adds bootcamp and AI training, the founder-side Delegation Mastermind, and Lifetime Replacement vs 6 months.
