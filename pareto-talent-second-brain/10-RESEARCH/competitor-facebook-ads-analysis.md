# Competitor Facebook ads analysis

Google Doc version: https://docs.google.com/document/d/1PMXsaXfj7b4x3rmkxR0rVCh0YueGhsuxghH9rexEMzA/edit (full, Parts 1 and 2; an earlier Part-1-only doc also exists). Feeds Part 8 of the Final Project (5 ads) with [[ad-structure]] and [[persona-pain-objection-language]].

## What the data is
Meta Ad Library snapshot, 2026-10-03. It has **no** likes, comments, clicks, spend or reach. The only performance signal is the "Low impression count" flag (<100 impressions), used here as a proxy for low delivery (not engagement). Meta's algorithm often starves some creatives early, so a flag can mean "not picked", not "disliked". Export order appears to put flagged ads last (inference). Ads found only for Pareto, BELAY and CloudTask; none for Viva, HireLATAM, South or Rose.

## Findings
**Pareto (low confidence: ads are 1-2 days old)**
- 15 static ads (Oct 2): same body copy and offer, only hook and headline differ; 5 delivered, 10 flagged. 7 near-identical video ads (Oct 3, "See details" button): all flagged. 2 Instagram boosted brand videos (Sep 13, "Subscribe"): not flagged.
- Delivered hooks: "I once paid a job candidate before they did any work." / "You don't have a systems problem. You have a people problem." / "7 steps. Every template. Free." / "Get the free book: HIRE." / "Hire people you never have to babysit."
- Flagged hooks: "You hired a second job." (x2), "Everything nobody taught you about hiring.", "Why does every assistant I hire end up needing me more?", "If you're on your third assistant...", "Wanna hear something embarrassing?", "Your last bad hire cost you months.", "Sunday night. You're rewriting an email your assistant drafted Friday.", "It's faster if I just do it myself.", "Still hiring whoever interviews best?"
- Tentative pattern: 7 of 10 flagged hooks open on a narrow negative situation; delivered ones lead with the offer or a bold reframe.
- 22 creatives launched at once with near-identical bodies, which splits early budget.

**BELAY (medium confidence: flagged ads are 1-4 weeks old)**
- Flagged (8): "Numbers handled. Trust earned." (x3), accounting-needs quiz, "Is It Time to Outsource Your Accounting?", "The one hire who touches everything", "Take The Draft Your Lineup Quiz", "There's an Opening on Your Roster."
- Pattern: 5 of 8 in bookkeeping/accounting (secondary line); abstract benefit headlines, no named person or proof; all use "Learn more" or "See details". None of the ~13 "Download"-button ads is flagged (weak signal).
- Delivered ads: named customer + concrete pain (Donita: 3 months of unsent invoices, books done on her anniversary vacation), tight niches (churches, pastors, RIAs, construction), guides with "Download".
- Tactics worth noting: ~13 guide ads (incl. 3 hook variants of [[belay-financial-advisors-delegation-guide]], e.g. "You're overwhelmed because you're over-involved."), ~6 quiz ads, client stories (Donita, Betsy, Tim, Leilani, Steven Jarvis), guarantee ad ("93% matched right the first time"), pricing and hours ads, "Apply Now" recruiting ads separated from buyer ads, creator endorsement (Donald Miller, "Comment BELAY"). Counts are keyword-based, approximate.

**CloudTask**
- Flagged: 2 of 4 copies of one cost-led AI-engineer ad (Sep 29): emoji checklist, headline "Engineers Now Available For Half The Cost".
- Long runners (3-3.5 months): retargeting videos "You have been looking at CloudTask. Now look at the talent.", with "Pay only if you hire" / "First 30 days free".

## Implications for Pareto's 5 ads
1. Fewer, more distinct concepts; duplicates split delivery.
2. Hooks that name a person, a pain and an outcome; reframe style fits delegation authority (BELAY's "Handing off a task is easy. Getting it back done right is where most advisors give up" is the same move).
3. "Download"-style button on lead-magnet ads.
4. One proof ad (client story with numbers) and one risk-reversal ad (guarantees).
5. Segment by persona, as BELAY does.
6. Verify in Ads Manager (CTR, CPM, cost per lead per hook), and re-export the Ad Library in about 7 days.

# Part 2: copy, creatives, videos, personas, angles, pains

**Scope and limits:** all 113 ads' full copy read. About 66 of 82 downloaded static creatives viewed (all 15 Pareto, 4 CloudTask, about 47 of BELAY's 63; one BELAY image sheet skipped). Frames sampled from 6 videos (2 Pareto, 1 CloudTask, 3 BELAY); the rest are text and duration only.

## A. Copy
- **Pareto (HIRE book):** all statics share one body (hook, "HIRE is Kasim's free book on finding people you don't have to manage", 3 bullets: job post, paid tryout, first 90 days; audiobook, templates, 3XFreedom seat; "Why free? We're not selling a book. We're building a community of founders who hire well."). Only hook and headline vary. Offer angle is hiring process, not delegation or freedom. Voice is Kasim first person; no client voice. Buttons: "Download" (statics), "See details" (videos).
- **BELAY:** diagnose-then-reframe ("You're overwhelmed because you're over-involved."); named-customer stories; niche call-out in line 1 ("Pastors, stop booking your own travel..."); guide ads naming asset and audience; process and objection ads (speed, hours, price, guarantee); sports metaphor campaign (lineup, roster, every position). Best RIA copy: "Handing off a task is easy. Getting it back done right is where most advisors give up... nobody has defined what finished looks like."
- **CloudTask:** retargeting ("You have been looking at CloudTask. Now look at the talent." / "Now meet Luisa.") with risk reversal; prospecting is a cost-led checklist.

## B. Static creatives
- **Pareto (15):** black and electric blue, HIRE book as hero, Kasim as face, no Pareto Talent logo. Formats: book mockups, template collage, native-UI imitations (iPhone Notes, ChatGPT chat, tweet card), text-only true-story card, desk photo. Delivered (5): babysit book stack, "people problem", "7 steps", template collage, true-story card. **All 5 creatives with no book visible were flagged (tweet card, Notes, ChatGPT chat, text-only "second job", tablet excerpt); with-book creatives delivered 5 of 10.** Low confidence (ads are 1-2 days old).
- **BELAY:** navy and green, logo, bold caps. Testimonial quote cards with headshot (Mahyar Khosravi, Betsy Ross, Leilani Sizemore, Carey Nieuwhof, Steven Jarvis); cartoons ("Your financial stress doesn't clock out"); guide cover mockups with a button; quiz cards; sports play diagrams; FAQ bubbles (cost, matching time); podcast quote cards; stock photos. One native-UI ad: Notes list "Things that came back to me this week: Rescheduled the Hendersons, twice / Fixed the CRM notes myself / Chased the transfer paperwork / Built the review deck at 9pm / Followed up because nobody else did." Flagged creatives I saw were generic (maze quiz card, FAQ "What does a BELAY Assistant do?", stock photo "Your business grew. Your systems didn't.").
- **CloudTask (4):** cost-led 1:1 ads ($15k+ vs $3k receipt, "Your next AI hire needs a new ZIP code", "U.S. hours. A different price tag").

## D. Personas, angles, pains
**BELAY personas (pain -> angle):** overloaded CEO/owner ("3 months of unsent invoices", books done on an anniversary trip -> client stories, "93% matched right first time"); financial advisors and RIAs (over-involved, approvals route through you, slow client response, work comes back half finished, CRM, meeting prep -> [[belay-financial-advisors-delegation-guide]]); pastors and church leaders (own travel, endless emails, church books are not business finances -> church-specific EA and bookkeeping); construction owners (never-ending to-do list, after-hours work -> podcast); sales leaders ("best closer stuck doing admin", "six figures for $20 tasks" -> guides); marketing leaders; small owners with bookkeeping stress ("financial stress doesn't clock out"); leaders who manage instead of lead (Leadership Playbook); job seekers (separate "Apply Now" ads).
**CloudTask personas:** SaaS sales leaders hiring reps; tech leaders hiring AI engineers (budget, speed, US time zone, trust).
**Pareto personas (as addressed):** founders burned hiring assistants: redo work at night, "faster if I just do it myself", assistants who need more management, hiring on gut feel or "whoever interviews best", a bad hire cost months, Sunday-night rewrites.
**Angles:** time back and leverage; over-involvement reframe; named-person proof; niche call-out; risk reversal; cost arbitrage (CloudTask); speed; self-diagnosis quizzes; personal match, not an algorithm; free asset with authority (Pareto).
**ICP read:**
- BELAY's buyer is a leader with a specific operational burden; trust and risk beat price (CloudTask is the cost-led exception).
- The strongest recurring pain is "work comes back wrong and I redo it", the same as Pareto's HIRE ads.
- No competitor speaks to founder freedom in a founder's voice.
- Pareto ads target a situation (burned by hiring); define the ICP also by revenue stage and team size so ads can be segmented (see [[icp]]).

## E. Changes to recommendations
1. Show the lead-magnet cover in every lead-magnet creative until data says otherwise.
2. One client-story ad (name, role, concrete result) and one niche ad.
3. Videos 30-45 s with the pain in the first 3 seconds; long cuts for retargeting.
4. Reconcile "91" vs "100" founders before any ad runs.
5. Retest native-UI formats with the book visible and more time (BELAY's Notes ad delivered).
