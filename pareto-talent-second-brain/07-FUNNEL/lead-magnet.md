# Lead magnet

Requirements: solves one specific founder problem in under 10 minutes; useful even if they never talk to Pareto; on-brand; delivered automatically by email; MAGIC title; pushes the Value Equation; leads to "I need a Right Hand."

## Evidence to choose from
- Top founder pains (1,500+ calls): admin 80%, no time 70%, burned by a VA 60%, runs from memory 50%, things slip 40%, too many hats 30%.
- Second brain is the "number one requested thing by entrepreneurs" — but Pareto already has a guide and a workshop on it.
- Pareto's funnel gap is MOFU/TOFU; the HIRE book is the only free gated magnet.
- Formats suggested in class: PDF guide, checklist, quiz, calculator, mini app.

## Candidate ideas (not decided)
1. **Delegation scorecard / quiz** — "Are you the bottleneck?" Scores the founder across the six pains and tells them what to hand off first. Qualifies naturally (revenue, team size).
2. **Founder hours calculator** — what doing it yourself costs at your hourly rate vs a Right Hand (uses Pareto's own 4.3x ROI logic).
3. **First-30-days handoff list** — the 25 tasks founders hand off first, by the six areas a Right Hand owns, with the staged-access plan.
4. **"Why your last VA failed" diagnostic** — for the 60% burned before; turns the objection into the hook.

Supporting research: ideas 1 and 2 match the strongest 2026 trends (interactive, qualifying; see [[lead-magnet-trends]], [[scorecard-marketing]]). Competitor clients' top trigger is "previous help failed" and their top pain is "doing it all", which supports ideas 1 and 4 ([[competitor-voice-of-customer]]).

Record the choice in [[09-DECISIONS/_MOC]].

## Ideas grounded in the HIRE book ([[hire-book]])
5. **"Are you delegating to the wrong who?" quiz**: built on the book's limiting beliefs; ends with "your delegation can only be as good as the who".
6. **Hire-it-yourself cost check**: the 7-step framework as a checklist with hours per step, showing the work Pareto already did.
7. **Success Pillars scorecard**: rate your offer to top talent on the five pillars.
Caution: the HIRE book already teaches founders to hire on their own; a new magnet should point to "or let Pareto do all 7 steps".
8. **"How many hours will hiring cost you?" calculator**: thousands of applications, 2 filter rounds, 5–10 trials, interviews, 90-day onboarding → founder hours plus trial costs; ends with "or Pareto already ran all 7 steps".
9. **Region picker (LatAm vs Asia vs Canada)** on the book's five factors; the book itself recommends Latin America for US companies.
10. **First 90 days with a new EA checklist** (12-for-10 pacing, daily check-ins, PTO, payroll, the 60-day milestone).
