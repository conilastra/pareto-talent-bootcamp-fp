# Client words, not marketer words

Rule: "Never invent the phrasing. Extract it." Lines marked (Ivan) are Ivan describing clients, not clients speaking.

## I'm the bottleneck
- "I'm the bottleneck in my own company." (sales calls, Day 8)
- "I become the full-time manager of just communications… a message router." (agency owner, Day 8)
- "where I'm the bottleneck like creating the videos" (Day 7)
- "The problem with most founders is that their context lives in their head, which makes them the bottleneck." (Ivan, Day 3)

## No time, no life
- "I built a company, and now I'm working in it as a job." (Day 8)
- "I wanted a business that would give me freedom, but instead, I work 12 hours a day, every day." (Day 8)
- "A lot of entrepreneurs work 12 hours a day, 7 days a week… they don't have life." (Ivan, Day 1)
- "no breathing room" (Day 9)
- "CEO, ops manager, scheduler, firefighter, all at once. Growth feels like a trap." (Day 1 slide)

## Drowning in admin
- "They are drowning in admin." (Ivan, Day 1)
- "instead of doing what you're actually good at… you end up figuring all of the stuff around it, all of the invoices, all of the contracts." (Ivan, Day 1)
- "20,000 of emails in their inbox" (Day 9)
- "I'm doing everything myself. I have a lot of manual processes" (agency owner, Day 8)

## Things fall through the cracks / runs from memory
- "things fall through the cracks… chase invoices… opportunities, referral emails… they start missing things." (Ivan, Day 1)
- "A lot of things for entrepreneurs runs from their memory." (Ivan, Day 1)
- "I know that when somebody messaged me on WhatsApp, I have this template…" (Day 3)

## Burned by a VA
- "already tried executive assistant, a virtual assistant, and this virtual assistant failed" (Day 8)
- previous VA "did not sound like them. They sent AI fluff." (Day 8)
- "A previous hire created more management work than it saved. No vetting, no accountability." (Day 1 slide)
- Past VAs "ghosted… not replying… delivery is trash." (Day 1)

## Letting go / control
- "it's their baby… afraid that somebody will break it" (Day 3, Day 9)
- "They're struggling with letting things go, they're struggling with delegation." (Ivan, Day 3)
- "I will do it myself, because it's faster, and it will be better." (Day 1)
- "you will not have the full context, you will not know how to reply" / "you don't have my voice, you won't be able to write like me." (Day 3)

## What they want
- "somebody who will feel like a partner… who will be their right hand." (Day 1)
- "real help… who will actually take things out of their plate, and not just bring things to their plate." (Day 1)
- "I want to ensure that this person can sound like me, because they will be speaking in my voice" (Day 8)
- "somebody who's vetted, who's proven, who's managed, so they will not spend their HR resource on that" (Day 8)
- "we need ads tomorrow" (Day 7)

## After (from the Wall of Love)
- "I can't imagine going back to like pre-Paula, or pre-an EA." — [[bo-royal]]
- "It's only day 2 and I am already feeling such a relief…" — [[daneen-goncalves]]
- "I just can't even imagine not having my EA right now." — [[jon-vroman]]

## Limiting beliefs founders hold (HIRE book, verbatim)
- "If you want it done right, you have to do it yourself."
- "No one cares as much as I do."
- "You can't rely on anyone these days."
- "Good help is hard to come by."
- "It's impossible to find someone who's willing to go the extra mile."
- "frazzled, exhausted, and overwhelmed—surrounded by a team of duds waiting around for direction"
- "How do you train and manage someone who's better than you? … Will they hold you hostage?"
(Source: [[hire-book]]. These are the beliefs Kasim quotes to argue against.)
- "It's either that or stick with the traditional model and hire someone first—then play the fun game I call: 'Let's find out if you suck at your job.'" ([[hire-book]])
- "Shortlisting the trial projects is easily the most arduous part of the entire process." ([[hire-book]])

## Competitor clients (NOT Pareto clients; from [[competitor-voice-of-customer]])
Use as language patterns, never as Pareto testimonials.
- Doing it all: "We all think as business owners that we can just do it all." (BELAY client) · "My mindset before was that I need to do it all... it was slowing me down and costing my business money." (DonnaPro client) · "We work in the business too much and not on the business." (BELAY client)
- Email: "My number one issue that I probably struggle with more than anything else is emails." · "By the time I would get to my emails, it would be in the evening, and that would take away from my family time." (BELAY client)
- Breaking point: "I was up at 2 a.m. researching flight options and connection times." (BELAY client) · "we had to turn down new projects because I was completely bogged down by administrative bottlenecks." (DonnaPro client)
- Burned before: "I've tried other administrative support programs from temps that are local to one that was an offshore virtual assistant, and this is the only thing that's worked for me." · "My history with Virtual Assistants goes back almost eight years and it's been a rollercoaster" · "That was a dumpster fire." (BELAY clients)
- What they want: "I want somebody who can see around corners. I want somebody who's ahead of me." (BELAY) · "I'd rather pay more for someone really good who I don't have to micromanage." (DonnaPro) · "I want to give my wishlist to someone, and then all the goodness would come to me" (BELAY)
- After: "I pay for peace." · "You can't put a price on the peace of mind." · "She's an extension of me." · "it's resolved before I even find it." (BELAY clients) · "I finally have the headspace to focus on what matters." · "my first real holiday in years" (DonnaPro clients)
- Value math: "If you're not doing $50 per hour work and doing $10,000 work instead, it doesn't take too many hours to see how that math adds up." (BELAY client)
