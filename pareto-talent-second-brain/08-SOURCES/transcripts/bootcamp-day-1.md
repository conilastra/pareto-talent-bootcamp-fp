## Frameworks and models taught

### 1. Find. Train. Match. (the Pareto model)
"We find talent. We train them. We place them with entrepreneurs." Ivan: "and support them from now on. That's the whole model."
1. **Find**: screen thousands for the top 1%: fluent English, real experience, drive to learn fast.
2. **Train**: 10 days live, then 40+ more hours "before you touch a real business".
3. **Match**: join the Pareto Talent Network. Talent is matched to a U.S. founder on "personality, work ethics, values and goals", with ongoing training, coaching and support.
- "This is not just a course. It is a full program with a training program in front of it."
- There is no fixed graduation quota ("We don't have a limit at, like, 50 seats... What we are looking for is the best"). The pool lets Pareto "match entrepreneurs better."

### 2. The talent process (5 stages)
Application (done; a 45-min application) → Bootcamp Training (10 days live) → Final Project → Join Pareto Talent (CSM interview, Talent Pool, weekly calls) → Get Hired (start with a U.S. founder).
- Placement mechanics: when a founder comes in, Pareto scans the pool and presents **up to three candidates**. The founder chooses. Timing ranges from before the bootcamp ends to 3+ months later. Fit is prioritised over speed "because if... the entrepreneur is dissatisfied, there is nothing we can do in order to save this relationship."

### 3. Founder vetting / intake (client side)
Founders "need to go through our intake process... onboarding process... take personality tests... submit a lot of materials that would allow us to match them." They are "not just coming to a marketplace and choose whoever." They interview Right Hands only after this process ("pre-vetted entrepreneurs").

### 4. Assistant vs Right Hand
| An Assistant | A Right Hand |
|---|---|
| Waits to be told what to do. | Notices what is missing before anyone points at it. |
| Finishes the task and stops. | Closes the loop and reports back without being chased. |
| Says "you didn't tell me to check that." | Sees the problem and has already drafted the fix. |
| Works from a job description. | Works from the outcome the founder actually needs. |
| Says "I don't know how to use that tool." | Learns the tool, then shows the founder how it works. |
- Pareto uses "executive assistant", **never "virtual assistant"**, and defines the role as a **Right Hand**. An assistant who waits to be prompted is "not much better than AI". If a founder "could ask AI to do the same thing... sooner than later, you will be replaced." "Being able to act unprompted is what defines us as humans."
- Ampi's JD "changes every single week": "today I need Facebook ads, tomorrow I need to rent a place for 80 people, and day after that, we need to publish a book."

### 5. The six areas a Right Hand owns (most run all of them within 3 months)
1. **Inbox & calendar**: inbox to zero, calendar defended, the week shaped in advance. Ivan says this is "going away" because AI can do it, so the Right Hand "use[s] AI on their behalf" and builds the agent.
2. **The Second Brain**: "One place holding everything the business knows. You build it, you keep it current." It uses AI to keep everything in one place: extract from calls, connect to Claude, run routines, build dashboards. Day 3 covers it in full.
3. **Projects & follow-through**: turn broad goals into plans and chase open loops.
4. **Systems & automation**: find repetitive work and build what does it.
5. **Content & comms**: draft in the founder's voice (emails, posts, decks, client updates), including personal brands, podcasts, books and talks.
6. **Client-facing work**: talk to the founder's clients and vendors on their behalf "without needing a script".

### 6. A real week (example tasks)
1. Monday **Morning Brief** before the founder opens the laptop: priorities, blockers, calendar conflicts resolved.
2. **Inbox pass**: "Clear 10,000+ emails to zero."
3. **Build request**: "We need a page for the webinar by Thursday", shipped by Wednesday with copy, design, VSL and ad script.
4. **After a client call**: pull the **Fathom** transcript, extract commitments, send follow-up in the founder's voice.
5. **Recurring drag**: automate a weekly report. "Tell nobody until it has run twice clean."

### 7. The three expectations (all ten days)
1. **Communication**: "Silence reads as a problem." Confirm receipt, commit to a time, flag slips first. "A short update on time beats a perfect update that arrives late." "The founder should never have to wonder what you are working on." Over-communicate. Avoid non-English abbreviations (e.g. "IA" for AI).
2. **Proactivity**: "Do not wait to be told." "Bring the answer, not the question. If you must ask, bring options with it." "A task is not done until the person who asked knows it is done." Flag broken things even if they are not yours. "You are not being hired to follow instructions. You are being hired to take work off a busy founder's plate."
3. **Technical self-reliance**: "We are not your 24/7 tech support." Search, read, try, then ask in #Questions showing what you tried. "The tools will change. The habit of figuring them out is what we are actually training."

### 8. The Four Cs of Communication
1. **Clear**: "Write so that you cannot be misunderstood, not just so that you could be understood."
2. **Complete**: "Include every piece of context the reader needs to act without replying to ask."
3. **Concise**: "Cut anything that does not change the decision. Length is not effort."
4. **Call to Action**: "End with the next step, a solution, or a specific ask. Never end open."
- Bad example: "there was an invoice in your account, I didn't know if you wanted me to do anything about this." Good example: "I received this invoice. I'm attaching it here. As I checked, it's from this client that is already paid. I think it's a duplicate. Should I go ahead and refund him?"
- "Communication is the job... being able to clearly answer and ask is, like, half the job."

### 9. The E.O.D. (end-of-day) report
- **Done**: what you finished today. **In Progress**: what you did not finish, and why. **Blockers**: what you need from someone else. (Optional) next up and on hold.
- "A couple lines, five minutes. The difference between being trusted and being managed." Link every item to the actual deliverable.
- Purpose: it is not time tracking. It is for setting priorities and staying in sync, and it gives the founder proof of work. It counters the "burned by a VA" belief: founders then feel "they don't need to record the screen... ask you to do a selfie in front of the computer every 2 hours."

### 10. Kasim's principles (quoted by Ivan)
- **"Forgiveness is better than permission"**: better to apologise for being too proactive ("I built you a new website because I thought you will need that") than to ask permission.
- **"If I delegate to you 10 things and you do 9 of them perfectly, but you forget about one of them, you are useless to me."** If you missed one, "chances are you missed more."
- **Close the loop**: every task or project is an open loop. "Your message should be the last one," even if it is "okay, I got it, thank you so much."

### 11. Professional vs unprofessional (bare minimum)
Use your full name, properly capitalised and matching your application (no "johnny doe"). Use respectful complete sentences (no "Sup bro"). Use a tidy, well-lit, current photo (not a cat or a 2015 photo). Use a short email address containing your name. "Webcam and mic are basically your modern age suit." Ivan: if you call "from the dark... they think that you're a cheap VA."

## Offer, guarantee and terms
**For talent (the Right Hand offer).** "Not a gig. A serious job, with terms we put in writing."
- Starting salary **$1,000+/month**, full-time only (no part-time). It is a floor, and pay is higher for relevant experience or niche fit (medical background, German, developer). Raises are based on performance and client satisfaction, and Pareto handles them.
- **20 days paid vacation (PTO)**, written into every client contract. It accrues at 1.7 days/month and accumulates within the year.
- Paid in **USD** via **Wise** or **Payoneer**.
- **No spyware or tracking**: no screen recorders, keystroke counters or activity scores. There are two exceptions where the EA volunteered. "We are not a VA or BPO company" with quotas and screenshots.
- Equipment support after 90 days with a founder, with the cost split between founder and Pareto (e.g. MacBooks). Tools are covered (Claude, Lovable, Canva, CapCut), up to $100/month.
- Contracts are signed with both talent and clients, and the handbook governs. "Pareto Talent is a U.S. company, so it's a U.S. LLC."

**Guarantee: Lifetime Replacement.** "If a placement does not work out, that is on us to fix." Ivan: it "works both ways". If a founder can't work with the Right Hand, Pareto replaces them, and the talent is re-placed, "as long as you are still compliant with our handbook, and you wasn't fired for something stupid."

**For founders (client offer elements mentioned).** Matched, pre-vetted, AI-trained Right Hands from a Talent Pool with up to 3 candidates presented. Founders also get:
- a **full Second Brain build-out**,
- a **founder community** with a weekly 90-minute session teaching "how to delegate better, how to work with remote talent",
- client success managers,
- the lifetime replacement guarantee.
- **Client pricing was NOT disclosed on Day 1.**
