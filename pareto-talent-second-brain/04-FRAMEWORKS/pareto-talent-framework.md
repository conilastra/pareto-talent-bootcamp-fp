# Pareto Talent Framework (7 steps to hire)

The book's core method. It "looks backwards" by design: paid tryouts come before interviews ("Job interviews? Worthless until you've actually worked with someone.").

1. **Clarify Core Principles** — six principles: people are miracles, not commodities; hire smarter than you; location doesn't matter; tenacity and will beat credentials; win-win; AI amplifies the right people. "Hire for will, train for skill."
2. **Create a Job Posting** — a sales letter, not an HR doc; see [[job-post-anatomy]] and [[flytraps-and-skunks]].
3. **Plan Distribution** — weigh five factors: Language, Infrastructure, Workforce Specialization, Time, Culture. Stay broad on geography, niche on job boards. LinkedIn is the only big board worth it; no Upwork/Fiverr ("They don't want a job—they want a client."). One "+" email alias per board to track sources. Latin America favoured for US companies: time zones, and "a Latin accent could remind them of their next door neighbor."
4. **Filter the Stack** — two rounds. Binary: 100% of instructions followed or archived ("Always archive, never delete"; automate with Gmail filters, Zapier or a GPT). Qualitative: lazy, misaligned, messy or not a fit, judged by a person or "a well-trained GPT". Leaves 5–10 per hire. "By the way, you shouldn't be doing this work at all. Your EA should."
5. **Run the Tryouts** — no first interviews. A paid trial under 2 hours at $10–20/hr, paid up front, built as a condensed real week, full of flytraps ([[flytraps-and-skunks]]). Expect 3–5 finalists per 10 trials. "Don't jump the gun and hire someone straight out of the trial project phase."
6. **Conduct the Interviews** — candidate picks the tool ("Treat me like a client…"); open with the trial project; four questions (5-year perfect world, overqualified, what if it doesn't work after 60 days, each skunk); review the personality test; tell them where they stand; offer. Non-negotiables for entrepreneurial hires: 2+ years, no stealing clients or staff, find and train a replacement.
7. **Onboard for Success** — contractor agreement; set the pace (assign 12 units for 10, keep a backlog, 8-hour days); 20 days PTO (1.7/month); pay on time (Payoneer > Wise > PayPal, cover fees); daily check-ins then weekly team and company meetings; announce the 60-day pass; team spirit (All-Hands, Open Mic ≤10 min, pods); be the incubator. "Hiring isn't the finish line—it's the starting gate."

All seven steps are from the book itself ([[hire-book]], full read).

**Why it matters for the funnel:** this is the work a founder skips by hiring through Pareto. Every step is a reason to book the call: "or skip all 7 steps; Pareto already ran them."
