# Six-part ad structure

**Hook → Offer (with risk reversal) → Qualifier → Proof → Mechanism → CTA (one only).** Compress all six.
- Persona by cause → several genuinely different angles (Part 8 needs 5 different messages, not 5 hooks on one).
- Continuity: the ad's promise must match the landing page.
- Meta = billboard (demand generation); Google = store they walked into (demand capture). Use standard targeting: "Your ad will tell Facebook who you want."
- Metrics: CTR, conversion rate, CPC (Pareto US ≈ $3.80), CAC, ROAS, LTV:CAC > 3:1. Start with a ~$1,000 test budget.
- Hook samples: weak "Do you need an executive assistant?"; MOFU "Have you wondered how you can make your agency make more money and spend less time?"; TOFU "There is one way for you to spend 80% less time and make 5 times more money."
