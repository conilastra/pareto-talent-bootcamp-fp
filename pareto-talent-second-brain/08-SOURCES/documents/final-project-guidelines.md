# Final Project guidelines (summary)

- Build a lead magnet for qualified founders + the funnel: landing page, qualifying form, booking page (qualified), thank-you page + nurture (not qualified). Everything should lead to "I need a Right Hand, and Pareto has one for me."
- Due end of day October 7 (2026). One PDF: Cover, Link Index (L1–L15, H1–H9), Walkthrough (Parts 1–9). Named `FP_FirstnameLastname_ParetoBootcamp.pdf`. Submit at bootcamp.paretotalent.com/finalproject.
- Every link must open publicly without login; no shortened links; missing items written as "L3 · -".
- Part 1: Second Brain trained on Pareto with ≥3 source types (paretotalent.com, Wall of Love, Day 1 story, bootcamp frameworks; ≥1 transcript, 1 document, 1 public page); 3 competitor lead magnets (format, promise, audience, capture and qualification, post-opt-in); ICP + 3+ personas by cause in client words.
- Part 4: format, promise (<10 min), MAGIC title + Value Equation, qualification criteria, next-step offer.
- Part 5: genuinely useful, on-brand, live, emailed automatically.
- Part 6: hero, what's inside, who it's for, proof with real Pareto numbers, CTA with form; qualified → booking page with calendar; not qualified → thank-you page delivering the resource.
- Part 7: pipeline (opted in, qualified, call booked, client + unqualified); opt-in workflow (deliver, tag, opportunity, notify self); qualified follow-up; unqualified nurture; booking confirmation + reminder; test end to end. **Email counts conflict:** doc says 2 qualified / 3 unqualified; slide says 3 / 1.
- Part 8: 5 Facebook ads (hook, offer, qualifier, proof, mechanism, CTA), 5 different messages; on-brand AI imagery.
- Part 9: 2-minute Loom.
- Scoring: 5 categories × 5 points; 20+ = certificate + Talent Pool interview. "The brief is the floor, not the ceiling."
