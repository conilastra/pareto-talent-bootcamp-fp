# The founder whose growth outran their systems

## Cause of overwhelm
What worked at a smaller size breaks as the business grows. Growth, not a breakdown, is the trigger (about 30 BELAY stories, 4 of 5 Boldly stories, most CloudTask stories, and a Pareto client).

## What they say (verbatim, with source)
- "We've grown really fast and were adding new staff at all different levels." (Boldly client)
- "Just the constant follow-ups with the clients... trying to have somebody sign the contract, follow up with them." (JV, Advance Soccer Training, Pareto client; grew from 50 to 200 students a week)
- "We were at a point where we had to turn down new projects because I was completely bogged down by administrative bottlenecks." (DonnaPro client)

## Situation and trigger (why now)
Growth outran the founder's capacity and the manual processes.

## What they've tried
Doing the follow-ups, contracts and scheduling themselves.

## Objections
"Nobody else understands how we do it." (JV: matching kids by level was "a unique process").

## What "freedom" means to them
Someone who learns the process, automates it, and lets them open the next location or launch the next offer.

## Best lead magnet angle
Which processes to hand off first as the business grows.

## Best ad angles
1. "Your business doubled. Your week didn't."
2. "Stop being the person who follows up."

## Qualification signals
Revenue growing, team growing, manual processes still run by the founder.
