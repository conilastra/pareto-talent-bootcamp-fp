# Established firm avoiding HR burden

## Cause
They can afford help but not the time and risk of recruiting, vetting, training and managing it.

## What they say
- wants "somebody who's vetted, who's proven, who's managed, so they will not spend their HR resource on that" — plus a replacement guarantee.
(Pareto's example was a Mexican firm; our funnel targets US founders.)

## Objections
Bad-hire risk; onboarding time; management overhead.

## Freedom means
A senior person shows up trained and Pareto carries the risk.

## Lead magnet angle
True cost of hiring an EA yourself vs. vetted and managed (calculator).

## Ad angles
1. "1 in 1,000 applicants. 40+ hours of training. Replaced for free if it doesn't work."
2. "No recruiting. No training. No management headache."

## Qualification signals
Team 10+, revenue well past the floor, hiring timeline now.
