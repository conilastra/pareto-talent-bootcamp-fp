# Flytraps and skunks

**Flytraps** — tests hidden in the job post that reveal capability before any resume: the adjective in the title, "Remote", the subject line "I actually read the instructions", a PDF resume named `Lastname.Firstname.ROLE-NAME.pdf`, the PI personality test, a CEFR English test, an animal question, a Gibran quote; in tryouts, wrong login credentials, no deadline, a payment process to navigate, a video explanation, stripped references, and the **pay-then-wait** test (send the money, hold the brief, see who follows up: "the second most diabolical flytrap"); in interviews, cancelling the call to see the reaction.

**Skunks** — deal-breakers stated upfront so the wrong people self-select out. Always: fully remote setup and a sixty-day trial.

The same idea works for a lead magnet's qualifying form: state who it's not for, and let the form filter.
