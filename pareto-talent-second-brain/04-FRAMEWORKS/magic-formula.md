# MAGIC naming formula

Name an offer or lead magnet with 3–5 of these:
- **M**agnet — an attractive theme or hook
- **A**vatar — who it's for, in the name
- **I**nterval — a timeframe or number
- **C**ontainer — the format word (Bootcamp, Blueprint, System, Challenge, Scorecard, Calculator)
- **G**oal — the outcome, the destination

Example from class: "Get your life back from your business in 10 weeks challenge." Exercise names: "The 20-Hour Owner", "Get Your Life Back From Your Business in 10 Weeks Challenge" (classroom exercise, not a Pareto product).

Required in Part 4 of the final project.
