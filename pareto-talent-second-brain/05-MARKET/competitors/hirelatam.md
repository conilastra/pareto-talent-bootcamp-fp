# HireLATAM

Recruiting service for pre-vetted remote Latin American EAs for US companies. Headline: "Hire an Executive Assistant from Latin America" — "3 to 5 pre-vetted remote Executive Assistant candidates in 5 to 10 business days".

- **Price:** flat "$3,500" per successful placement; no monthly fees or salary markup (founder pays the EA directly; optional payroll support).
- **Process:** Define scope → We source and vet (English, role fit, remote readiness) → You interview 3–5 → Hire and onboard.
- **Guarantee:** "90-day replacement guarantee… we source a replacement at no additional placement fee."
- **Proof:** "500+ hires"; typical hire in "2 to 3 weeks"; video testimonials (Boaz Rauchwerger, CEO Boaz Power; Jason Adams, Signal Ventures; Aaron Gianni, Larratt Bros Plumbing).
- **Capture:** "Four quick fields" form; CTAs "Get 3 to 5 vetted EA candidates", "Start Hiring"; "We will follow up within 1 business day." No gated lead magnet found.

## Pareto vs HireLATAM
HireLATAM is a recruiter: one fee, then the founder employs and manages the EA alone, with a 90-day guarantee. Pareto is a managed program: training (bootcamp, AI), employer of record, CSM, weekly founder mastermind, Lifetime Replacement. Objection to prepare for: "Why pay $3,600/month when I can pay $3,500 once?" Answer from Pareto's own material: "Not a placement. A complete delegation system." / "There's nothing more expensive than a cheap employee." / founders burned by unmanaged hires (60%+).
