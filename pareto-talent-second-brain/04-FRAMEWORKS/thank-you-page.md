# Thank-you page (4 elements)

1. **Confirmation** 2. **Next steps** 3. **Support** 4. **Objection-handling** (kills buyer's remorse: case studies, testimonials)
Best version delivers value immediately (time to value). Doing nothing after a form "is a fatal mistake." For booked calls: call prep, proof and hype reduce no-shows.
