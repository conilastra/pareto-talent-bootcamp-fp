# BELAY — Executive Assistant ROI Calculator

A cost-angle magnet: a downloadable Google Sheet that turns the EA cost into an investment. Logged from the lead magnet trends research; form fields and follow-up were not captured.

## Why it matters for Pareto
- It is BELAY's cost magnet, next to its readiness magnet ([[belay-delegation-worksheet-guide]]). BELAY keeps them separate; combining them is the gap ([[lead-magnet-trends]]).
- A spreadsheet download is static: the founder has to do the work. An on-screen calculator with an instant result is the stronger version.
- BELAY's own clients use the same math: "If you're not doing $50 per hour work and doing $10,000 work instead..." (Brad Johnson, [[competitor-voice-of-customer]]).

## The workbook (copy in 08-SOURCES/raw-files/belay-ea-roi-calculator.xlsx)
**Sheets:** Instructions, Task calculator, Results Key.
**How it works:** copy the sheet; enter annual salary and hours worked a week (the template shows $250,000 and 55 hours as an example → "true hourly rate" $87.41, calculated as salary ÷ 52 ÷ hours); enter weekly hours against **34 EA-able tasks** in five groups (Administrative 9, Operational 8, Financial 6, Personal 5, Marketing 6): inbox, calendar, travel, voicemail, CRM, expense reports, invoicing, social media and so on. Monthly cost = hours × 4.2 × hourly rate. Outputs: hours a month, cost each month, cost each year.
**Results Key:** hours a month (30-, 31-45, 46-55, 55+) and cost a month ($1,000-, $1,001-2,000, $2,001-3,500, $3,501+), as a read-out of how much the time costs.
**CTA inside the file:** "We said it might surprise you. If you're ready to reclaim those hours and that salary, schedule a call today!"
**Formula observations (read from the file, which is a blank template; not tested in Excel):** the hours and cost totals (monthly and yearly) add rows 20-44 only, which leaves out the nine Administrative tasks (inbox, calendar, meeting prep, travel and so on) from those totals, while a separate total adds all rows; and one row (calendar management) multiplies by hours instead of the hourly rate. If confirmed in Excel, the yearly figure understates the cost of exactly the tasks BELAY sells first.
## Strengths and weaknesses
Strength: a concrete, personal cost number built from the founder's own tasks; the most complete task list we found (34 tasks). Weakness: a static spreadsheet the founder must copy and fill; BELAY captures no data and gets no lead; no qualifier; the sales step is a sentence at the bottom of the sheet.
## What Pareto can do better
Make it an on-screen tool (not a download), keep the task-by-task hours idea but shorter, capture the email before the number, qualify, and route.
