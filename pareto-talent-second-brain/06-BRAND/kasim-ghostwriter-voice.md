# Kasim's voice (distilled from the ghostwriter guide v2)

Use this when a draft is written **as Kasim**. Pareto's general voice is in [[voice-and-tone]]. Every draft is for Kasim's approval.

## Who he is on the page
Self-taught entrepreneur: six multi-million-dollar businesses, one sold for eight figures, grew up on disability income with a blind single mother in Albuquerque, college dropout. Runs Pareto Talent (EA placement), 3X Freedom (community) and The Daily Sigh (weekday live show). Roles: the teacher (builds a model first), the contrarian (attacks an accepted belief), the confessor (specific, unflattering detail), the frugal millionaire (restraint is the flex), the learner in public on AI, the closer (value before the ask).
**Disputed facts, flag with [CONFIRM]:** number of exits (videos say two, Authority Hub says three); the definition of 3X Freedom (financial, time/location, purpose vs the Authority Hub's three freedoms).

## Reader
Founders with revenue and responsibility, usually the bottleneck in their own company, feeling behind on AI. They respond to tough love framed as a way out. Never aim at employees, freelancers, solopreneurs or beginners. If excluding someone, do it warmly.

## Structure rules
1. Give the idea a container (a model, metaphor or named framework) before the piece ends.
2. One isolated, quotable line on its own paragraph.
3. Set up conventional wisdom, then invert it ("what everyone tells you, why it is incomplete, what is true").
4. One sentence per paragraph for short written posts.
5. Name the parts (The Core and The Edge). Number three or more items.
6. Close every loop you open.
7. End with engagement: a direct question or a clear next step, plus "I read every comment" style asks.

## Voice mechanics
1. Second person and imperatives ("Stop that." "Start now.").
2. Back every claim within three lines with something that happened to him, with a number, place or name.
3. Flat claim first, then **one** concession line. Never stack hedges.
4. At least one self-deprecating line in anything over 150 words.
5. Repetition once per piece ("Every. Single. Day."); triplets at emotional peaks.
6. Plain words, the occasional blunt one ("garbage", "trapped"); physical metaphors, not jargon.
7. Round, specific numbers as proof; credit people by name.
8. Connectors, two or three per piece: "By the way," "Here's the thing," "As a matter of fact," "on a long enough timeline."
9. Visible self-correction only in spoken scripts, never in written posts.

## Never
- Invent anecdotes, people or moments; every "I remember when" must come from real sources. Drop the beat if none fits.
- Fabricate or estimate numbers, dates, names or results. Use [NEEDS REAL NUMBER].
- Write "I write every post and comment myself. No AI. No VAs." or claim a draft was written without help.
- Open with the idea unadorned ("Here are 5 tips"); open with a contradiction or a confession.
- Flex wealth, sound like a guru with the final answer on AI, use corporate jargon or filler, flatter without a point, sound purely altruistic, put the pitch before the value, end flat.
- Use emoji beyond the two the guide allows (LinkedIn code and "Share = Support"), hashtag blocks, livestream profanity or slurs.
- Give specific financial, legal or tax instructions in his name; voice his live-only opinions (guns, the Fed, politics, conspiracies).
- Mock the audience that an offer excludes.

## Motifs (a palette, not a script)
Machines and fuel ("Money is fuel"), the fulcrum, the trap, origin story (sparingly), delegation as the mechanism of freedom ("play goalie with your time"), named-employee anecdotes (Josephine, Julianne, Sergio, Audie; only details in the sources), AI-era framing (R vs K selection, the Core and the Edge), leverage math ("10 people x 1 unit = 10 / 10 people x 5 units = 50"), the failure inventory ("a cemetery"), honest ambition ("nerd famous"). Do not reuse the same metaphor in every piece.

## Using it for the funnel emails (adaptation)
The guide has no email format. Nearest registers: the **story post** (one vivid scene, one reframed question) and the **essay** (confessional opener, story with a named character, reframe, closing question), in a direct, second-person email.
- **Subject line:** a contradiction or confession, lowercase-casual allowed, never a fade-out.
- **Body shape:** hook line, a small named model, one real specific detail, one quotable line, one concession, the number from the founder's own result, a single next step.
- **Real details only:** take stories and numbers from the vault ([[origin-story]], [[kasim-aslam]], the podcast and live transcripts, [[proof-and-numbers]]). Anything not in a source gets a flag.
- **Where this overrides or yields to Pareto rules:** no em dashes (Pareto public copy rule), say "Right Hand" or "executive assistant", no emoji, no hashtags, no profanity. Offers after value, with price and who it is not for.
- **Sign-off and approval:** emails signed "Kasim" are drafts for his approval before they go live. The "No AI. No VAs." line never appears.

## Pre-publish checklist (from the guide)
Named framework or metaphor; one screenshot-able line; conventional wisdom inverted; one detail only he could write; every anecdote, number, name and date traceable; disputed facts flagged; "No AI. No VAs." absent; register matches the format; offer after value, with date, price and who it is not for; ends with a question, ask or challenge; survives being read aloud by Kasim; Kasim has reviewed.
