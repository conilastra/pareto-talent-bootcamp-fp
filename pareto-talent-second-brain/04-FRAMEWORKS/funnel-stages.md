# Traffic and funnel stages

- **Chain:** Traffic → Landing page → Form → CRM. "Everything after the click is what actually runs the business."
- **TOFU / MOFU / BOFU:** BOFU = Google Ads, referrals, warm email. MOFU = Meta and LinkedIn ads, problem-aware search. TOFU = YouTube, podcasts, social. People enter at any stage.
- **Pareto's gap:** BOFU converts but "there is just, 3 of them" — they need MOFU and TOFU. A lead magnet is a MOFU asset.
- **8 break points:** sees ad → clicks → page loads → reads → clicks CTA → submits → thank-you page → tracked conversion.
- **Ticket-size rule:** under ~$300 → checkout; above → education or a call (Right Hand Program = call).
- **Outbound/Inbound × Cold/Hot** quadrants.
