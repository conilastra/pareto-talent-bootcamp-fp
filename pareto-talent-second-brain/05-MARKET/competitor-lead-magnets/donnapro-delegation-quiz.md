# DonnaPro — delegation quiz

## Format
5-step quiz, marketed as a 30-second audit. CTA "See My Results". Conditional logic tailors the result.
## Promise
Whether hiring an EA fits "how you actually work".
## How they qualify
Built into the quiz: role, team size and timeline are the qualifiers. This is the closest competitor structure to what Pareto's brief asks for (a magnet that qualifies).
## What happens after (verified)
1. The quiz branches by **team size** to a result page:
   - Small team: https://donnapro.com/quiz-free-breakdown/step-2-small-team/ with the headline "Your Quiz Says You're Managing 2-10 People - But You're Still the Bottleneck for Everything".
   - Big team: https://donnapro.com/quiz-free-breakdown/step-2-big-team/ with the headline "Your Results Are Clear: You're Managing More People But Getting Less Done Than Ever".
2. The result page is a **segmented headline plus proof**, not a score: 91% client retention, 110+ industries, 70+ task types, 7+ years, Google and Trustpilot ratings, client logos, 10+ video and text testimonials, press (Forbes, Inc., Entrepreneur). No form, no email.
3. One CTA, "Schedule Your Free Strategy Session" → https://donnapro.com/step-3/.
4. **/step-3/ booking form** ("Claim Your Free 30 Minute Strategy Session", on Google Meet): first and last name, email, phone, company website, role (CEO/MD/chairman/founder/co-founder or other, with a description if other), a yes/no commitment question ("would you invest in a top 1% executive virtual assistant in the EU if it could save you 60 hours a month?"), optional notes. No testimonials on this page. This is the qualifier: it screens for decision-makers and commitment.
Not seen: confirmation email, reminders, any follow-up if the founder does not book. The page reader could not show the quiz questions themselves (the questions listed above come from the quiz page text).

## Takeaways for Pareto
- Closest competitor structure to the brief: quiz, qualifying questions (team size drives the branch), a result page, then a booking form that asks role and commitment.
- Strength: no friction before the call; the form questions filter tire-kickers; the headline matches the reader's team size ("you are still the bottleneck").
- Weakness: no email captured until booking, so anyone who does not book is lost (no list, no nurture, no resource). It gives no personal number or score, and only two result variants.
- Pareto can keep the segmentation and the booking qualifier, but also capture the email first, give a personal result with hours back, and send unqualified founders to a thank-you page, the resource and nurture, as the brief requires.

## Also: ungated content
30 guides (cost by country, "When to Hire an EA", "The CEO's Delegation Playbook", industry pages for law firms, real estate, healthcare, e-commerce, private equity, a 12-point agency checklist, ROI calculator inside the cost guide). All web articles, no gate.
## What Pareto can do better
Show the score on screen with a specific number of hours, route qualified founders to the Matching Call calendar, and speak to US founders rather than EU CEOs.
