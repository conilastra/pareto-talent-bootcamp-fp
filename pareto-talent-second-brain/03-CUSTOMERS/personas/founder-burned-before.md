# The founder who was burned before

## Cause of overwhelm
An earlier temp, freelancer, offshore VA or agency hire failed, so a new hire means more management, not less. "Previous help failed" is the top trigger in the research (about 40 BELAY stories, plus DonnaPro, South and Boldly).

## What they say (verbatim, with source)
- "I've tried other administrative support programs from temps that are local to one that was an offshore virtual assistant, and this is the only thing that's worked for me." (BELAY client)
- "My history with Virtual Assistants goes back almost eight years and it's been a rollercoaster in terms of success." (BELAY client)
- "That didn't work out. That was a dumpster fire." (BELAY client, on hiring alone)
- "I've had three different executive assistants before, and I thought it was me, right? I was burning through them." (Eric Ritter, Pareto client)

## Situation and trigger (why now)
The last help failed, or left, and the work piled back up.

## What they've tried
Temps, freelancers, Upwork, other VA agencies, offshore VAs, hiring alone.

## Objections
"It will be the same again." "It's faster to do it myself."

## What "freedom" means to them
Someone proactive, "ahead of me", who needs no micromanaging.

## Best lead magnet angle
A diagnostic for why the last hire failed and what to check before hiring again.

## Best ad angles
1. "It wasn't you. It was the setup." (no vetting, no training, no management)
2. "1 in 1,000 applicants. Replaced for free if it doesn't work."

## Qualification signals
Has had at least one assistant or VA before; team of 2 or more; wants a managed option.
