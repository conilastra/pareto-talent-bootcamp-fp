# Voice of Customer: South (hireinsouth.com) and DonnaPro (donnapro.com)

Collected 2026-10-03 for Pareto Talent competitive research.

**How this was collected / caveats**
- Every page was read with WebFetch (curl blocked). WebFetch passes the page through a small extraction model, so text was requested "verbatim, character for character". Quotes below are what that tool returned. They have not been checked against a raw HTML dump. Spot-check any quote before you publish it.
- South: the Stories listing (https://www.hireinsouth.com/blog-categories/stories) has **100 post URLs** and no pagination. All 100 were opened. One fetch reported "103 cards", but a full URL enumeration returned exactly 100 unique posts.
- DonnaPro: one page (https://donnapro.com/testimonials/). It has no linked case-study pages. The only related links are comparison/SEO articles (/comparison/...), which are not testimonials and were not used. There are 3 video testimonials. **The videos were NOT transcribed.** Only the caption/pull-quote text printed on the page was captured.

# PART 1 - SOUTH (hireinsouth.com)

## Key finding
South's "Stories" are **case studies written by South in the third person, not client testimonials.** Across 100 posts:
- **0** named clients (every client is anonymous, e.g. "an AI startup" or "a U.S.-based lighting rental company")
- **1** attributed client quote (hotel chain, CS Specialist, quoted below)
- 2 near-quotes written by South in its own narrative voice (HR Manager; BDR)
- **0** videos with client testimony (a few pages embed a Beehiiv newsletter form, not testimony)
- **0** objections or hesitations voiced by clients. One page mentions a regulatory issue: the energy company had concerns about hiring in Venezuela, which South's outsourced (EOR) model solved.
- Almost all posts use the same template: Client / Challenge / Requirements / Candidate / Cost (LATAM vs US) / Time to hire / Results.
