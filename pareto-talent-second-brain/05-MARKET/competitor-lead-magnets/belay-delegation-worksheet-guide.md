# BELAY — Delegation Worksheet & Guide

**Two URLs (verified by Conil):** the original page gives the guide directly with no data asked; the hub page asks name and email with an optional phone. The earlier "12-field form, phone required, SMS opt-in" description (from a page read) does not match what Conil saw and should not be cited.

## Format
Downloadable worksheet and guide.
## Promise
"What if you could delegate just five tasks to save yourself 15 hours every week? Sounds counterintuitive, but the less you do, the more you can accomplish."
## Who it's for
Leaders and entrepreneurs who struggle to delegate.
## How they capture
Two versions (verified by Conil): the original page gives the PDF with no data asked; the hub page asks name, email and an optional phone. (An earlier page read recorded a 12-field form with phone required and SMS opt-in; that was not reproduced.)
## How they qualify
Not in the flows tested.
## What happens right after opt-in
The PDF itself (7 pages). The older notes described a thank-you page with a 4-step process (consultation call, matching, meet your team, start delegating), more downloads and an FAQ, with no booking calendar; that page was seen on an earlier read and is not confirmed in Conil's tests. Email sequence: not seen.
## Strengths
Heavy qualification, filters out job seekers (Pareto faces the same with its talent audience), strong time promise (5 tasks → 15 h/week), thank-you page sells the process.
## Weaknesses
Generic worksheet; self-scored on paper, so no instant personal result; no qualification; the PDF has no booking link.
## What Pareto can do better
Give an instant, personal result (score or hours number) in under 10 minutes; ask 3–4 questions max (BELAY asks almost none); send qualified founders straight to a calendar; add the "job seeker" filter because Pareto's bootcamp audience is large.


## The PDF (7 pages, received 2026-10-03; copy in 08-SOURCES/raw-files/belay-delegation-worksheet-guide.pdf)
Same three exercises as the financial advisor version, generic: **#1 The 80/20 rule**; **#2 The 6 T's** (Tiny, Tedious, Time-consuming, Teachable, Terrible at, Time-sensitive); **#3 Delegation matrix** (love/hate by important/unimportant → Keep, Release, Trust, Forget), with explanations of each box. Last page "Your trusted partner": "BELAY has a pool of over 2,000 vetted, U.S.-based professionals", three benefits (vetted professionals with industry experience; responsive and managed U.S.-based support; flexible and scalable service), "Delegate the details to us, so that you can get back to what you do best — leading." Tagline "Elite support for leaders who refuse busy work." No booking link or price in the PDF text. Self-scored on paper: no calculation, no personal result.
**Note:** the "promise" (5 tasks, 15 hours a week) is on the landing page; the PDF itself teaches method only.
