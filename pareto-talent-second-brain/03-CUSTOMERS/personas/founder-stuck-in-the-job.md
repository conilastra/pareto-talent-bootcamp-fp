# Founder stuck in the job (candidate)

## Cause
70% "no time, no life"; 30% wearing too many hats; ~70% work nights and weekends.

## What they say
- "I built a company, and now I'm working in it as a job."
- "I wanted a business that would give me freedom, but instead, I work 12 hours a day, every day."
- "CEO, ops manager, scheduler, firefighter, all at once. Growth feels like a trap."

## Lead magnet angle
Find the hours: audit where the week goes and what to hand off first (time audit / delegation calculator).

## Ad angles
1. "You didn't build a business to become its most expensive employee." (participant headline, Day 6)
2. 80/20: "20% of your time generates 80% of the outcome. What do you do with the other 80%?"
