# Speaker / coach / consultant who needs their voice

## Cause
Content, email and client comms must sound like them. Past help produced generic AI output.

## What they say
- "I want to ensure that this person can sound like me, because they will be speaking in my voice"
- Previous VA "did not sound like them. They sent AI fluff."
- "you don't have my voice, you won't be able to write like me." (Day 3)
- "where I'm the bottleneck like creating the videos" (Day 7)

## Objections
"No one can write like me"; inbox access; trust.

## Freedom means
Content and comms go out in their voice without them writing it.

## Lead magnet angle
Capture your voice and context so someone else (and AI) can write like you: voice profile / second-brain starter.

## Ad angles
1. "Your assistant shouldn't sound like ChatGPT."
2. Content bottleneck: "You're the face, not the editor."

## Qualification signals
Revenue floor, ongoing content/client volume, ready to share access in stages.
