# ICP

**Draft paragraph (built from Pareto's own words and the research):**
Pareto's ideal client is a US founder or CEO of a founder-led small or medium business. It is for founders doing at least $500K a year in revenue who have a team but no one who can take whole areas off their plate. They are personally the bottleneck: they work nights and weekends, run their inbox, calendar and follow-ups themselves, and many have already been burned by a cheap or unmanaged assistant. They do not buy tasks. They buy hours back, nothing slipping through the cracks, and an operator who is vetted, trained and managed, so they never have to recruit or manage one.

## Pareto's own statements
- Slide (Day 8): "Founders running a business past a real revenue floor, who are drowning in admin, and who are personally the bottleneck."
- Ivan (Day 3): "small and medium businesses in the United States. Primarily founder-led companies. Usually making at least $500,000 a year. Typical sales cycles is approximately 2 weeks."
- Day 2 (Kasim's ideal reader): "a founder of the company… $500,000 to $5 million… annual revenue… within United States."
- Day 8 market: "at least, like, $500,000" revenue, "not a solo startup"; ~3M businesses TAM, ~1M ICP.
- Day 6: "if their company size is one person, then they cannot delegate to anyone."
- Origin: "built this company for solo founders… founder-led company… like Kasim. You need a right hand who will run with you as fast as possible." (Day 8)
- Website: "Seven- and eight-figure founders". The /matching video says clients are founders doing $100K+ a month, which is an example of who Pareto serves, not a minimum.
- Webinar: founders at $50K–$1M per month.

## Industries seen
Agencies, coaches/speakers/consultants, real estate, AV integration, multi-company founders, personal brands (podcasts, books, speaking), lawyers (emerging niche, 5–10 placed), an established 50-person Mexican firm. Trusted by Genius Network, Lifestyle Investor, Front Row Dads, Driven Mastermind.
