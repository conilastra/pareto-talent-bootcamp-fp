# DonnaPro

Executive assistants for CEOs and founders, **EU-based** (Portugal, Spain, Italy, Poland, Romania; 19+ country pages). Targets growth-stage founders, established CEOs and multi-venture leaders in tech, finance, legal, real estate, e-commerce, healthcare ("110+ industries"). Promise: reclaim ~60 hours a month; "Save 15 Hours A Week, Guaranteed".

- **Price (homepage, 2026-10-03):** part-time €2,700/month (most popular); full-time €6,500/month; €1,000 onboarding fee shown as waived "through 26.07.2026" (date already past, stale banner).
- **Process:** free 30-minute strategy session → "HR Time Vault" (30-day, six-level vetting) → matched, onboarded in about 9 business days → "Freedom Phase".
- **Guarantee/proof:** 60-day zero-commitment trial; 91% retention after the trial; 99% applicant rejection; 7+ years; 70+ task types. Named-CEO written testimonials and videos (see [[competitor-voice-of-customer]]).
- **Lead magnets:** a 5-question quiz that branches by team size to a result page, then a free strategy session booking form (no email before the call), and 30 ungated guides → [[donnapro-delegation-quiz]]. 
- **CTAs:** "Get started", "Claim Your Free 30-Minute Strategy Session".
- **Vs Pareto:** Not LatAm, so a different time-zone and price band (€6,500 full-time is far above Pareto's $3,600). Closest in tone: founder and CEO pain language, delegation guides, and a quiz. Its guides by industry (law firms, real estate, healthcare, e-commerce, PE) show the niche-page tactic Pareto's lawyer niche could copy.
