# Kasim Aslam

Co-founder of [[company-overview|Pareto Talent]]. Built and exited Solutions 8 (#1 Google Ads agency; 8-figure exit 2022). 15 years hiring EAs. Author of *HIRE* ([[thehirebook]]), *You vs Google*, *The 7 Critical Principles of Effective Digital Marketing*. Founder of 3X Freedom; co-founder of Driven Mastermind; co-host of Perpetual Traffic podcast. Hired 300+ remote employees. AZIMA Interactive Person of the Year (2017); Top 50 Digital Marketing Thought Leaders in the US.

- Consulting: $2,000/hr (Day 5). Library of 14,000 books (Day 8). Self-described "ADHD entrepreneur" with "a ton of the ideas all the time" (Day 5).
- Voice: "short sentences, very direct." Channels: YouTube, podcasts, Facebook, LinkedIn, Instagram, books.

- Was in the War Room mastermind with Alex Hormozi (Day 5).
