# About Pareto Talent

## Origin story
Kasim Aslam built Solutions 8 into the #1 Google Ads agency worldwide. In 2018 he hired Ivan Bunin from Freelancer.com when Ivan was 18. Ivan started as an executive assistant, became Tech Lead, then CTO, and ran the operational and financial due diligence for the company's 8-figure acquisition in 2022. After a failed self-storage startup, Kasim and Ivan partnered to create Pareto Talent: first placement April 2024, $1M in revenue by May 2025, 100% referrals, zero ad spend.

## Mission
"Place 1,000 executive assistants with founders — to help entrepreneurs stop being the bottleneck in their own businesses, and to give talented people around the world the same opportunity Ivan got when Kasim took a chance on a 18-year-old from a freelancer site."

## Three core beliefs
1. **People Are Not Commodities**: above-market pay, meaningful work, flexible remote arrangements.
2. **We Train Both Sides**: the EA and the founder both learn delegation.
3. **We Eat Our Own Cooking**: the founders run the system internally.

## Team named
Kasim Aslam (co-founder), Ivan Bunin (co-founder), Ampy (Ivan's EA, first internal hire).

## Quote
"We didn't build another staffing agency. We built the program we both wish had existed — one that trains the EA and trains the founder."
