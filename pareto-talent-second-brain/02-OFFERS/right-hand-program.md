# The Right Hand Program

"Pareto Talent's placement service that matches founders with a full-time elite remote operator who owns outcomes rather than tasks." ([[kasimaslam-right-hand-program]])
Headline on /matching: **"Reclaim 40+ hours a month with a pre-trained Right Hand."** "No recruiting. No training. No management headache."

## What the founder gets (value stack, homepage)
1. An AI-powered Right Hand (1 in 1,000 applicants), full-time, your time zone, fluent English
2. The Delegation Mastermind — weekly live 90-minute call with 100+ founders
3. Pareto Right Hand Community — 250+ operators (peer support for your Right Hand)
4. 10-day AI bootcamp + continuous training (104+ sessions/year)
5. [[guarantees]] (three, or four on kasimaslam.com)
6. Pareto Second Brain OS (decisions and SOPs system)
7. Dedicated Client Success Manager

Also mentioned: core AI tool subscriptions covered (kasimaslam.com; Day 1 says tools covered up to $100/month), Pareto is employer of record and handles contracts and USD payroll, no spyware or BPO quotas, 20 days PTO written into the contract.

The stack grew from what clients asked for: placement → community → ongoing training → equipment, apps, subscriptions (Day 5).

## What a Right Hand does → [[assistant-vs-right-hand]]
Six areas owned within ~3 months: inbox & calendar; second brain; projects & follow-through; systems & automation; content & comms in the founder's voice; client-facing work. Trained on Claude, ChatGPT, N8N, Canva, CapCut, Lovable, Operator AI, HubSpot, HighLevel, ClickUp, Asana. Matched on strengths with Kasim's 2-6-2 lens (Day 7).

## How you get one
Homepage: watch a short video → answer quick questions → book the **Matching Call** → 3 hand-picked candidates within 24 hours → interview all three, choose → 30-day integration.
kasimaslam.com: Apply (questionnaire + behavioral assessment, ~45 min) → Discovery call → Matching (~24 h) → Intro call (optional trial task) → Commit (contract and payment after selection).
Matching uses the Predictive Index (PI) behavioral assessment on both sides. Sales cycle ~2 weeks (Day 3).
