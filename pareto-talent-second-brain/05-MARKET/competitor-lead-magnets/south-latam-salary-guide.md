# South — Latin American Remote Salary Guide

## Format
Gated downloadable guide; the opt-in is a newsletter form.
## Promise
Salary benchmarks by role and industry for US companies hiring in Latin America. "Latin American salaries are 30-80% less than US-equivalents."
## Who it's for
US companies scaling with LatAm talent.
## How they capture
Email via Beehiiv embed. CTA: "Discover salary benchmarks by industry and role for US companies hiring today".
## How they qualify
They don't on the form; qualification happens later on a sales call.
## What happens right after opt-in
Guide by email and newsletter [to verify]; "Schedule a call" CTA with "Free to interview, pay nothing until you hire."
## Strengths
Very low friction; answers a concrete question buyers have (what does it cost?); cost-savings hook.
## Weaknesses
No qualification; attracts price shoppers; positions talent as cheaper, not better.
## What Pareto can do better
Pareto argues against the "cheap labor" frame ("There's nothing more expensive than a cheap employee"). A Pareto magnet should sell outcome and time back, and qualify on revenue before offering a call.
