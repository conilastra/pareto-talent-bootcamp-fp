# AI image and video production

"Iterate, don't regenerate." Always give three inputs: section description, style guide, palette screenshot. Ads: break down a working ad into a reusable master prompt, generate in batches by pain point, finish in Canva ("AI plus templates equals a good image"). B-roll priority: your own footage > AI footage of your likeness > stock. Mix and match hooks and bodies. Tools: Higgsfield, HeyGen, OpusClip, CapCut, Canva, Claude Design.
