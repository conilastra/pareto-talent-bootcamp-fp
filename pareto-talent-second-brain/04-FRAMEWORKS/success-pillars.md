# Five Success Pillars and the high-water mark

**Five Success Pillars** top talent looks for: Compensation, Community, Purpose, Freedom, Growth. (Cheat sheet: "Rate your offerings" on each.)

- **High-water mark rule:** pay at least 10% above the highest typical pay for the role in that area. Example: Kasim paid a Philippines EA $1,500/month when peers paid $400.
- **Freedom:** "Be as flexible as the role allows." 77% value flexible work over a raise (People Management poll, 2,000+).
- **Retention:** you don't retain Pareto Talent. Two side-hustle rules: no competing; train your replacement. "Be an incubator, not a container."
- **AI ladder:** AI amplifies whatever quality a person has. "Congratulations—you've now scaled mediocrity to the ends of the earth."
