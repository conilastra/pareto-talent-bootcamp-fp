# 4-Gate Idea Test and 7-Factor Pareto Score

**4-Gate Idea Test (fast kill):** one message (no "and"), pain point in the viewer's words, broad appeal, curiosity hook.
**7-Factor Pareto Score (0–3 each):** View Potential, Proven Concept, Intrigue, ICP Resonance, Payoff ("five-dollar test"), Pre-Selling Power, Brand Alignment (veto). 16+ ships; 12–15 repair and rescore; ≤11 bank it.
Scripting findings (slides): hook length doesn't matter; numbers in the hook underperform; aim negation at the reader ("You're doing X wrong").
