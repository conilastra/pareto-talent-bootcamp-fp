## How to find competitors (Day 8)
Ask AI; check who Kasim interacts with; search "Kasim Aslam speaking" for co-speakers (e.g. Ad World); a real competitor fights "for the same customer… same target addressable market". Outsource Accelerator's directory lists Pareto among LatAm EA staffing agencies, a good place to find peers.

## Patterns across competitor lead magnets
| | Athena assessment | BELAY worksheet | South salary guide | Magic eBook |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Format | 10-question quiz | worksheet + guide | salary guide | 100-task eBook |
| Promise | where your delegation gaps are | 5 tasks → 15 h/week | LatAm salaries, 30–80% less | what an EA can take off your plate |
| Friction | low (email at the end) | high (12 fields, phone) | low (email) | low |
| Qualification | none | revenue, intent, service, industry | none | none |
| Personal result | yes, a level by email | no | no | no |
| After opt-in | result + "Find your EA match" | thank-you page with 4-step process, consultant call | newsletter + "Schedule a call" | eBook by email |

**The opening for Pareto:** Athena already proves the quiz format works in this market, but it doesn't qualify and doesn't route anyone to a call. BELAY qualifies hard but gives a generic worksheet behind a 12-field form. Nobody combines an **instant on-screen personal result** with **2–3 qualifying questions** and **routing qualified founders straight to a calendar**. Viva, Boldly and Rose have no lead magnet; Viva relies on comparison pages. HireLATAM has an interactive JD + salary tool ([[hirelatam-job-description-salary-tool]]), CloudTask an ungated guide library ([[cloudtask-guide-library]]), and South a free email delegation course ([[south-10x-delegation-course]]), the only magnet in Pareto's delegation territory. Every competitor magnet is cost-led or generic and none qualifies before the call.
