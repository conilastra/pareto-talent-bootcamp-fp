# Pareto jargon

- **Right Hand** — Pareto's term for its placed operator. Also "executive assistant / EA". Pareto never says "virtual assistant". See [[assistant-vs-right-hand]].
- **Right Hand Program** — the core paid offer. [[right-hand-program]]
- **Matching Call** — the sales call booked from /matching.
- **Talent Pool / Pareto Talent Network** — trained Right Hands available for placement.
- **Bootcamp / cohort** — free 10-day training for applicants (cohort #7 in Sept 2026); doubles as recruitment.
- **Final Project** — the bootcamp's graded build; clients are told talent "passed the final project".
- **CSM** — Client Success Manager.
- **Delegation Mastermind** — weekly 90-minute live session for Pareto clients on delegation.
- **Second Brain / Second Brain OS** — a founder's knowledge base (context layer + action layer). [[two-layer-second-brain]]
- **Morning Brief** — daily brief the Right Hand prepares before the founder opens the laptop.
- **EOD report** — Done / In Progress / Blockers. [[eod-report]]
- **Close the loop / open loop** — a task isn't done until the asker knows; your message is the last one.
- **Four Cs** — Clear, Complete, Concise, Call to Action. [[four-cs]]
- **Freedom 40** — guarantee: 40+ hours back in the first 30 days or next month free. [[guarantees]]
- **Lifetime Replacement** — free replacement any time. [[guarantees]]
- **Founder2Founder** — Kasim or Ivan personally step in (kasimaslam.com).
- **Wall of Love** — testimonials page.
- **Structural trap** — a business that depends on the founder (homepage).
- **Message router** — what an overloaded founder becomes (client words, Day 8).
- **"Forgiveness is better than permission"** — Kasim's rule for Right Hands.
- **Client words, not marketer words** — extract phrasing from calls, never invent it.
- **AI slop / AI fluff** — generic AI output; Pareto positions against it.
- Kasim-isms the AI pulled from his content (Day 2, unverified meaning): "the Pareto rule", "filtration process", "Pay 10% above the high water mark", "fly traps", "the graveyard", "borrowed pride".

## From the HIRE book ([[hire-book]])
- **Pareto Talent (fractal 80/20)**: elite contributors who deliver 10x–100x their peers. [[pareto-principle-fractal]]
- **Pareto Talent Framework**: the 7-step hiring method. [[pareto-talent-framework]]
- **Flytraps / skunks**: hidden tests in a job post / deal-breakers stated upfront. [[flytraps-and-skunks]]
- **High-water mark**: pay at least 10% above the highest typical pay for the role ("Pay 10% above the high water mark"). [[success-pillars]]
- **Success Pillars**: Compensation, Community, Purpose, Freedom, Growth.
- **Tryouts / filter the stack**: paid trial before interviews / binary then qualitative filtering.
- **Utility players**: generalists; avoided except for EAs ("Their job is the ambiguity").
- **Needle in a stack of needles**, **key man risk**, **incubator not container**, **micro-frictions**.
- Still undefined in any source: "the graveyard", "borrowed pride", "7 Laws of Delegation", "Big Three", "Sovereignty Brief".
