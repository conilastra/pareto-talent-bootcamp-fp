# Qualification criteria

**Decision (2026-10-04, Constanza):** revenue cut-off is $500K a year. The other criteria are in the project's Part 4.

## Evidence for the cut-off
| Signal | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Revenue floor (ICP) | ~$500K/year | Day 3, Day 8, Day 2 |
| Revenue on /matching | $100K+/month (~$1.2M/yr), given as who Pareto's clients are, not a requirement (Conil, 2026-10-04) | [[paretotalent-matching]] |

| Team size | not solo ("cannot delegate to anyone") | Day 6, Day 8 |
| Geography | US founders | Day 3, Day 6 |
| Budget | required budget field in Ivan's booking form | Day 9 |
| HIRE form fields | company size, revenue, hiring timeline, pain points | [[thehirebook]] |

## Price reality check
The cheapest plan is $3,600/month + $3,000 fee (~$46K year 1). A founder below ~$500K/year would be spending ~9%+ of revenue. That supports a $500K/year floor at minimum.

## Candidate questions for the form (pick 2–4)
1. Annual revenue (tiers, cut at $500K)
2. Team size (solo vs 2+)
3. Where are you based? (US / other)
4. When do you want help? (now / 1–3 months / just exploring)
5. Monthly budget for a full-time Right Hand (≥ $3,600 or not)
