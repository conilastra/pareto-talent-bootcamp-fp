# Athena — CLEAR Framework Guide + Worksheet

## What it is
A separate Athena guide, not the quiz result (the quiz result is [[athena-quiz-result-email]]). CLEAR = Context, Limits, Expectations, Accountability, Review. Includes a "steal this" prompt template, two failure stories, a worksheet to list 15-20 professional and 15-20 personal tasks with hours per week, and a stage map of what Athena EAs take on in year one.
## Strengths
Useful on its own; a worksheet that totals hours per week (the time-back number); concrete before/after examples; no hard sell. Brand-consistent and shareable.
## Weaknesses
Generic to any leader; a generic asset, not personalised; no qualification; the only upsell is a course, not a call.
## What Pareto can do better
Pair a method like this with a personal result (Athena's quiz email does that) and a qualifier, and add an explicit "speak with Pareto" step for qualified founders. Note Athena also splits method (CLEAR) from sales (match flow).
