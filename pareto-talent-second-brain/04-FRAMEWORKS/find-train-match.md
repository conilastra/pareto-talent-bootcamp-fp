# Find. Train. Match.

"We find talent. We train them. We place them with entrepreneurs." Plus ongoing support.
- **Find:** screen thousands for the top 1 in 1,000 (English, experience, drive). Latin America.
- **Train:** 10-day live bootcamp + 40+ hours; final project; ongoing 104+ sessions/year.
- **Match:** PI behavioral assessment on both sides; up to 3 candidates; founders are vetted too ("pre-vetted entrepreneurs", "not a marketplace"). Kasim's **2-6-2** lens (Day 7): of 10 skills a person is bad at 2, okay at 6, great at 2; match the top 2 to a founder who needs them.
Talent process: Application (45 min) → Bootcamp → Final Project → CSM interview → Talent Pool → Hired.
