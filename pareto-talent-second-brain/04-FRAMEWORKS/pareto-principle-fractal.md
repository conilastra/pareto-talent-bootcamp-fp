# Pareto Talent (the fractal 80/20)

"Elite contributors who consistently deliver 10x, 20x, even 100x the results of their peers." The 80/20 rule repeats at every level: in 1,000 people, 8 produce 51.2% of the output and 1 person about 25%. This is where the company name comes from.

- "your delegation can only be as good as the 'who' that you're delegating to."
- "It's not that they're bad at delegating—it's that they're delegating to the wrong people."
- "There's nothing more expensive than a cheap employee."
- "So, now we're searching for a needle in a stack of needles."
