# Origin story

1. **2018** — [[kasim-aslam|Kasim Aslam]] runs Solutions 8 (6 employees at the time). He hires [[ivan-bunin|Ivan Bunin]], 18, off Freelancer.com for about $1,200/month as an executive assistant. Kasim's job description: "help me with projects, and finding dentists, and helping with emails." ([[bootcamp-day-1]])
2. **2018–2022** — Ivan grows from EA to Tech Lead to CTO. Solutions 8 becomes "the #1 Google Ads agency worldwide", 6 → 60 staff, 200+ clients.
3. **2022** — Solutions 8 is acquired (8-figure exit). Ivan runs the operational and financial due diligence.
4. **After the exit** — a self-storage startup fails. Kasim and Ivan partner to build Pareto Talent. Startup cost: $2,097 (Day 1).
5. **Late 2023** — Pareto founded. "Kasim spent 15 years hiring assistants and watching most of it fail; Ivan had been the one that worked." ([[bootcamp-day-1]] slide)
6. **April 2024** — first cohort and first placement. First client: Tim Bratton; "the 30-minute intro call ran 2.5 hours" (verify before public use).
7. **May 2025** — $1M revenue, 100% referrals, zero ad spend ([[paretotalent-about]]).
8. **2026** — ~$3M ARR, 100+ founders, 250+ Right Hands, bootcamp cohort #7.

**Why this story sells:** Ivan *is* the product proof. A Right Hand hired at 18 became CTO and partner. The promise to founders is that a Right Hand can become a strategic partner, not a task-taker.

Key line: "We didn't build another staffing agency. We built the program we both wish had existed — one that trains the EA and trains the founder." ([[paretotalent-about]])
