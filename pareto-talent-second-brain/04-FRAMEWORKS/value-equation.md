# Value Equation

**Value = (Dream outcome × Perceived likelihood) / (Time delay × Effort & sacrifice).** "Push the top up. Push the bottom down." "Fix the number, not the objection."

- **Dream outcome:** "'Save time' is weak. 'Get your inbox to zero by 9am, every day'…"
- **Likelihood:** "A number beats an adjective." Use real proof ([[proof-and-numbers]]).
- **Time delay:** "'See your first draft brief tomorrow morning' asks for almost none."
- **Effort:** "'Answer five questions, we handle the rest' sounds like relief."

**Use in the final project:** Part 4 asks how the lead magnet title pushes the Value Equation. A lead magnet should deliver a specific result in under 10 minutes (low time delay, low effort).
