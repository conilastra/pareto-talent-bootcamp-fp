# Athena — Delegation assessment

## Format
Free 10-question quiz, promoted on the homepage ("10 Questions · free", CTA "Take the free assessment").
## Promise
"How well do you actually delegate? Get a clear picture of where the gaps are, what's holding you back, and what it takes to delegate more effectively."
## Questions (paraphrased by the page reader)
1. Your last successful delegation: what made the difference? 2. A team member decides for you without checking: how do you feel? 3. You tried delegating to an assistant/contractor: what happened? 4. Two-week vacation, no phone: who covers? 5. A recurring task you meant to hand off: what's stopping you? 6. A peer pays for an EA: gut reaction? 7. A peer has an EA: first honest reaction? 8. How does your day start? 9. Last time you ended a day feeling you worked on the right things? 10. A year from now with delegation figured out: what's different?
## How they capture
Email gate after question 10, before results.
## How they qualify
They don't, in the quiz. No revenue or team-size question; everyone gets the same "Find your EA match" CTA.
## What happens right after opt-in
Result by email: one of six levels based on Athena's "5 Levels of Delegation" content, with barriers and traits. Email sequence: [to verify by taking it].
## Strengths
Instant personal result; low effort; questions surface the exact objections (burned before, "can I afford it", control); ties into their delegation content.
## Weaknesses
No qualification, so sales can't tell a $5M founder from a solopreneur; result arrives by email, not on screen; no booking step; generic levels.
## What Pareto can do better
Score on screen, add 2–3 qualifying questions (revenue, team size, timeline), route qualified founders straight to the Matching Call calendar, and personalise the result with what to hand off first and hours they could get back. Use Pareto's own pains (1,500+ calls) and frameworks rather than generic levels.
