# How Pareto sources, vets and trains Right Hands

- **Region**: Latin America only. Why: time-zone alignment with US clients, cultural fit, English fluency, bilingual, university-educated pool.
- **Volume**: 1,000+ applicants per cohort. **Acceptance**: <0.1% (1 in 1,000).
- **Vetting**: multi-week filtration with Predictive Index (PI) behavioral profiling. 93% retention at 12 months.
- **Training**: 40+ hours over 10 days.
  1. AI & Technology (10+ h): ChatGPT, Claude, N8N, Canva, CapCut, Lovable, Operator AI
  2. Operational Excellence (10+ h): inbox management, CRMs (HubSpot, HighLevel), ClickUp, Asana, SOP creation
  3. Delegation Frameworks (10+ h): proprietary "7 Laws of Delegation", escalation protocols, priority matrices
  4. Soft Skills (10+ h): professional English, cross-cultural dynamics, client communication
- **Final project**: multi-day autonomous simulation testing real-world decisions and problem spotting.
- **Matching**: PI profile + founder intake; AI recommends candidates; Success Manager curates.
- **Ready now**: 32 trained Right Hands available (at capture date).
- **After placement**: dedicated Success Manager, 104+ sessions library, 36 advanced courses, weekly live training on Mondays, 250+ member community with 24/7 support.
