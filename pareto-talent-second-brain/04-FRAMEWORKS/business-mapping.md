# Business Mapping (Day 10)

**What:** a flow chart of every step of a business, from the client's point of view, where each step links to the real asset behind it (page, form, SOP, email, automation, deck, contract, invoice). Ivan: it removes "the anxiety of having way too many moving parts". Final Project Part 2 is a Business Map of the lead magnet funnel; the deliverable is [[business-map]].

## How to draw it
1. Start left to right with **traffic sources**, only those the business really uses (Facebook, Google Ads, referrals, organic).
2. Landing page → form → **qualifier (diamond)**: qualified yes/no.
3. For each branch: which email, which automation, what happens next (sales call, booking page, nurture).
4. Keep drilling down; then **link every box** to its real asset.
5. Mark anything missing as a **gap** (red; green = built). Never invent a link.

Tool: Draw.io (free, in Google Drive). Miro, Lucid, Freeform, diagrams.net or Notion are all accepted for the project.

## Six-stage spine (any business)
| # | Stage | Slide "stages" |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Traffic: every source of strangers | 1 |
| 2 | Capture: landing page, form, first click (checkout, scheduler, quiz, lead magnet) | 2 |
| 3 | Qualify and nurture: who fits, what happens to those not ready | 3-4 |
| 4 | Sales, money, handoff: conversation, payment and paperwork, sold becomes delivered | 5-7 |
| 5 | Onboarding through 90 days: first week, first value, delivery rhythm, check-ins | 8-11 |
| 6 | Retain, grow, exit | 12-14 |

## The 10-second rule
For each step: can you name the document, process, link or automation within ten seconds? If yes, link it. If not, it is a **gap**: do not help them remember, do not accept "well, sort of". A fabricated link "destroys the one thing a map is actually for: being trustworthy enough to act on."

## Why it matters
- **Key-man risk:** a process only one person understands; if they leave, it falls apart.
- **Exit:** a documented, mapped business is one somebody else can buy ("Exit is the ultimate test").
- **Redesign on purpose:** move a step on the map and the process changes deliberately; Pareto re-checks its own map yearly and simplifies it.
- **For a new Right Hand:** interview the owners of each process, record the calls, let AI assemble the flowchart, hand it to the founder with the blanks marked.
