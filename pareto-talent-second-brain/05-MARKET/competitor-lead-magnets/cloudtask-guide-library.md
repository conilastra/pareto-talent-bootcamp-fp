# CloudTask — guide library

## Format
Five published guides: LATAM GTM Salary Guide 2026 (web article); AI Operator Playbooks for GTM Teams (web article); 2026 LATAM AI Talent Hiring Master Guide (12-page PDF, 50 roles, four countries); Why LATAM for GTM Hiring: The Business Case (8-page PDF); The CloudTask Buyer Guide (web article, five-step process and pricing). Four more "coming soon" (Remote Hiring Playbook, SDR Outsourcing Guide, LATAM Talent Market Report, SkillBridge Certification Guide). Also a swipeable `/compare` tool (CloudTask vs US direct hire vs US staffing agency), a newsletter and case studies. Earlier note: an ungated EA hiring guide embedded on the EA page, see [[cloudtask]].
## Promise
Cost, timezone, talent availability and retention evidence for hiring in LatAm.
## Who it's for
GTM and AI roles mostly (SDR, AE, CSM, RevOps); not founder EAs specifically.
## How they capture
They don't. Trade-off: maximum reach and SEO, no list.
## How they qualify
They don't; the qualifier is the "Get Matched" role brief.
## Strengths
Authority and trust through free, ungated depth; PDFs for sharing internally.
## Weaknesses
No email list from the content; GTM/AI focus, so little for EA buyers; cost and region framing.
## What Pareto can do better
Gate one asset that gives a personal result and qualifies; use ungated content only for SEO and proof. See [[competitive-landscape]].
