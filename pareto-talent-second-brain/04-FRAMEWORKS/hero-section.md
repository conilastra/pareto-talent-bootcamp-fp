# Hero section rules

Four must-haves, no exceptions:
1. **Title** — the outcome stated directly; not clever, not vague
2. **Sub-headline** — who it's for or how it works, whichever removes more doubt faster
3. **Social proof above the fold** — a number, logo or face
4. **Real video or imagery** — not stock

Clarity over creativity ("Ensure that you cannot be misunderstood"). Reading order: title → button → video → rest. **Squint test:** the CTA is the brightest element; one primary CTA. CTA text like "Reserve your spot", never "Submit".
Ivan's critique of Pareto's own hero: yellowish background, weak title contrast, generic image, too few questions answered. Model hero: the Second Brain Workshop page.
