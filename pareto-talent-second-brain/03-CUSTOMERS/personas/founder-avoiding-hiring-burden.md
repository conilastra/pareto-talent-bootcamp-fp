# The founder who does not want to recruit or manage anyone

## Cause of overwhelm
Hiring is slow, expensive and pulls the founder away from the business. Trust in someone far away is the second barrier.

## What they say (verbatim, with source)
- "I knew I wanted an agency to avoid the hiring hassle." (DonnaPro client)
- "I couldn't go out and find somebody like Leah or Hali, with their skill sets, to come to work with me." (BELAY client)
- "Trusting somebody from anywhere part of the world that is not close to you is tricky." (JV, Pareto client)
- "DonnaPro doesn't just connect you with an assistant and walk away... We didn't have to recruit, train, or worry about compliance." (DonnaPro client, Renato Lozinšek)

## Situation and trigger (why now)
They need help now and have no time to find, vet and train anyone.

## What they've tried
Hiring alone, or interviewing candidates and giving up.

## Objections
"Who is behind this person if it goes wrong?" Bad-hire risk; onboarding time.

## What "freedom" means to them
A vetted, trained, managed person who is ready to lead, with a company carrying the risk.

## Best lead magnet angle
The true cost in hours of hiring an assistant yourself, versus vetted and managed.

## Best ad angles
1. "No recruiting. No training. No management headache."
2. "Someone vetted, trained and managed, in 24 hours."

## Qualification signals
Wants a managed service, has no HR bandwidth, team of 2 or more.
