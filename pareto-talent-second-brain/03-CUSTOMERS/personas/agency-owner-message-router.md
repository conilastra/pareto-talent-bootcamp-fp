# Agency owner turned message router

## Cause of overwhelm
They are the hub for all communication and manual processes. Hiring a VA made it worse, not better.

## What they say
- "already tried executive assistant, a virtual assistant, and this virtual assistant failed"
- "I'm doing everything myself. I have a lot of manual processes"
- "I become the full-time manager of just communications… a message router."
- "I'm the bottleneck in my own company."

## What they've tried
A cheap VA or freelancer: "more management work than it saved. No vetting, no accountability."

## Objections
Burned before; "faster to do it myself"; cost.

## Freedom means
Clients and team get answers without them; they work on growth, not routing.

## Lead magnet angle
Diagnose what's routing through them and what to hand off first; how to avoid the VA mistake twice.

## Ad angles
1. "You didn't start an agency to become its switchboard."
2. Burned-by-a-VA: why it failed (no vetting, no training, no system) and what's different.
Day 8 MOFU hook sample: "Have you wondered how you can make your agency make more money and spend less time?"

## Qualification signals
Team of 2+, revenue $500K+, US, wants help now.
