# Competitor Facebook ads analysis

Google Doc version: https://docs.google.com/document/d/1YfCu7WdPKPOQlG1xG1INCzzV6jG9WCf5w40r7HerO-0/edit. Feeds Part 8 of the Final Project (5 ads) with [[ad-structure]] and [[persona-pain-objection-language]].

## What the data is
Meta Ad Library snapshot, 2026-10-03. It has **no** likes, comments, clicks, spend or reach. The only performance signal is the "Low impression count" flag (<100 impressions), used here as a proxy for low delivery (not engagement). Meta's algorithm often starves some creatives early, so a flag can mean "not picked", not "disliked". Export order appears to put flagged ads last (inference). Ads found only for Pareto, BELAY and CloudTask; none for Viva, HireLATAM, South or Rose.

## Findings
**Pareto (low confidence: ads are 1-2 days old)**
- 15 static ads (Oct 2): same body copy and offer, only hook and headline differ; 5 delivered, 10 flagged. 7 near-identical video ads (Oct 3, "See details" button): all flagged. 2 Instagram boosted brand videos (Sep 13, "Subscribe"): not flagged.
- Delivered hooks: "I once paid a job candidate before they did any work." / "You don't have a systems problem. You have a people problem." / "7 steps. Every template. Free." / "Get the free book: HIRE." / "Hire people you never have to babysit."
- Flagged hooks: "You hired a second job." (x2), "Everything nobody taught you about hiring.", "Why does every assistant I hire end up needing me more?", "If you're on your third assistant...", "Wanna hear something embarrassing?", "Your last bad hire cost you months.", "Sunday night. You're rewriting an email your assistant drafted Friday.", "It's faster if I just do it myself.", "Still hiring whoever interviews best?"
- Tentative pattern: 7 of 10 flagged hooks open on a narrow negative situation; delivered ones lead with the offer or a bold reframe.
- 22 creatives launched at once with near-identical bodies, which splits early budget.

**BELAY (medium confidence: flagged ads are 1-4 weeks old)**
- Flagged (8): "Numbers handled. Trust earned." (x3), accounting-needs quiz, "Is It Time to Outsource Your Accounting?", "The one hire who touches everything", "Take The Draft Your Lineup Quiz", "There's an Opening on Your Roster."
- Pattern: 5 of 8 in bookkeeping/accounting (secondary line); abstract benefit headlines, no named person or proof; all use "Learn more" or "See details". None of the ~13 "Download"-button ads is flagged (weak signal).
- Delivered ads: named customer + concrete pain (Donita: 3 months of unsent invoices, books done on her anniversary vacation), tight niches (churches, pastors, RIAs, construction), guides with "Download".
- Tactics worth noting: ~13 guide ads (incl. 3 hook variants of [[belay-financial-advisors-delegation-guide]], e.g. "You're overwhelmed because you're over-involved."), ~6 quiz ads, client stories (Donita, Betsy, Tim, Leilani, Steven Jarvis), guarantee ad ("93% matched right the first time"), pricing and hours ads, "Apply Now" recruiting ads separated from buyer ads, creator endorsement (Donald Miller, "Comment BELAY"). Counts are keyword-based, approximate.

**CloudTask**
- Flagged: 2 of 4 copies of one cost-led AI-engineer ad (Sep 29): emoji checklist, headline "Engineers Now Available For Half The Cost".
- Long runners (3-3.5 months): retargeting videos "You have been looking at CloudTask. Now look at the talent.", with "Pay only if you hire" / "First 30 days free".

## Implications for Pareto's 5 ads
1. Fewer, more distinct concepts; duplicates split delivery.
2. Hooks that name a person, a pain and an outcome; reframe style fits delegation authority (BELAY's "Handing off a task is easy. Getting it back done right is where most advisors give up" is the same move).
3. "Download"-style button on lead-magnet ads.
4. One proof ad (client story with numbers) and one risk-reversal ad (guarantees).
5. Segment by persona, as BELAY does.
6. Verify in Ads Manager (CTR, CPM, cost per lead per hook), and re-export the Ad Library in about 7 days.
