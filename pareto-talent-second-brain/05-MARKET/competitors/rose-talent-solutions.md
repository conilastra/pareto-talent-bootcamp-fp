# Rose Talent Solutions

"Hire an Executive Assistant": a full-time nearshore EA "who owns calendar, inbox, travel, and small ops." Colombia and other LatAm countries, US business hours.

- **Price:** "$2,500/mo flat. Month-to-month, 30-day notice. Free replacement if not a fit." Compared on-page to a US in-house hire at $5,800–$9,100/month loaded.
- **For:** executives spending 2+ hours a day on calendar, inboxes with 1,000+ unread, "losing Sundays to travel and expense reconciliation".
- **Process (7 days):** Day 1 scope call (10 min) → days 2–4 recruiter sourcing → days 4–6 role tests (calendar conflicts, inbox triage, travel booking) → day 7 matched and onboarded.
- **Vetting:** skills tests, English checks, references; "No personality quizzes" (a jab at assessment-based matching like Pareto's PI).
- **Proof:** average placement 7 days; "5+ Executive Assistants available this week"; Trustpilot 5.0; testimonials: Robert Delgrande (founder, ad spend $3K → $65K/month), Tanya Brooks (cut admin 30+ h/week), Marcus Whitfield (CEO, pipeline in under 60 days), Sarah Kendall ("Felt like a local employee from day one").
- **Lead magnet:** none found. CTA "Hire Talent" → /start.

## Pareto vs Rose
Rose is the price anchor: $2,500/month vs Pareto's $3,600/month + $3,000 fee, and a week to place vs Pareto's 3 candidates in 24 hours. Pareto must justify ~$1,100/month more with what Rose doesn't offer: 1-in-1,000 selection with a 10-day bootcamp and AI training, behavioural (PI) matching, the founder's weekly Delegation Mastermind, Freedom 40 and Lifetime Replacement, and 93% retention at 12 months. Prepare for the objection "Rose is $2,500."
