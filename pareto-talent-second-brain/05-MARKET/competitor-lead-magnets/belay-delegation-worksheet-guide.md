# BELAY — Delegation Worksheet & Guide

## Format
Downloadable worksheet and guide.
## Promise
"What if you could delegate just five tasks to save yourself 15 hours every week? Sounds counterintuitive, but the less you do, the more you can accomplish."
## Who it's for
Leaders and entrepreneurs who struggle to delegate.
## How they capture
Long form, CTA "Access now". Phone required; SMS opt-in.
## How they qualify
"I want to Hire BELAY" / "I want BELAY to hire me" (filters job seekers), annual revenue dropdown, which assistant type, industry. International visitors get a notice of requirements.
## What happens right after opt-in
Thank-you page "Your life is about to get exceptionally better." with 4 steps (Consultation Call, Matching Process, Meet Your New Team, Start Delegating), more downloads (Ultimate Delegation Playbook, Guide to Working with an EA, 25 Marketing Tasks…, Outsourced Accounting guide) and an FAQ on onboarding, matching guarantee, pricing. No calendar embedded on that page. Email sequence: [to verify by opting in].
## Strengths
Heavy qualification, filters out job seekers (Pareto faces the same with its talent audience), strong time promise (5 tasks → 15 h/week), thank-you page sells the process.
## Weaknesses
12 fields is a lot of friction; generic worksheet; no instant personal result; no booking calendar on the thank-you page.
## What Pareto can do better
Give an instant, personal result (score or hours number) in under 10 minutes; ask 3–4 questions max; send qualified founders straight to a calendar; add the "job seeker" filter because Pareto's bootcamp audience is large.
