# Personas (by cause)
Four personas, built from the research on competitors' clients plus Pareto's own client words. Each quote is labelled by source; competitors' clients' words are language patterns, never Pareto testimonials.

- [[founder-who-has-to-do-it-all]]: cause: delegating feels like failing, so everything runs through them.
- [[founder-burned-before]]: cause: an earlier assistant or hire failed.
- [[founder-growth-outran-systems]]: cause: growth outran the founder and the manual processes.
- [[founder-avoiding-hiring-burden]]: cause: hiring is slow and risky, and trust in someone far away is hard.

Emerging niche (not a persona): lawyers.
