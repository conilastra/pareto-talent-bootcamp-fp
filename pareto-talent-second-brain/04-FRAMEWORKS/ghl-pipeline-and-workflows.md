# GoHighLevel pipeline and workflows

- **Contact vs opportunity:** "A contact is an entry in your phone. An opportunity is the specific deal you're actually working with them."
- **Pipeline stages** named in past tense. Taught: New Lead → Qualified → Booked → Showed Up → Enrolled (slide variant: Registered → Attended → Booked a call → Client → Churned/Lost). Final project needs: opted in, qualified, call booked, client, + unqualified stage.
- **Automation blocks:** Trigger → Condition (If/Else) → Action → Delay/Wait. Filter the trigger to YOUR form (common disqualifier).
- **Graded workflow:** Form submitted → tag → opportunity → internal notification (to yourself) → email sequence. Tag naming `product::action_initials`.
- **Live pattern:** If/Else on revenue; wait 10 min; if no booked tag → "you forgot to book" email.
- **Five-beat sequence:** Thank You → Hype → Reminder → Launch Day → Urgency.
- **Pareto's real nurture:** one triggerless "Lead Nurture" workflow every other workflow hands into; emails every 7 days for about a year; exits on conversion tags.
- Email benchmarks: open 20–40%, click 2–5%, unsubscribe <0.5%.
- **Five automation pitfalls (Day 4):** no error handling, no idempotency, no human checkpoint, tool scope creep, no test run.
