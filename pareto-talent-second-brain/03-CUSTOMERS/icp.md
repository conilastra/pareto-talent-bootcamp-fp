# ICP

**Draft paragraph (built from Pareto's own words):**
Pareto's ideal client is a US founder of a founder-led small or medium business, usually past $500K a year in revenue (7- and 8-figure founders on the website), with a team but no one who can take things off their plate. They are "drowning in admin", work nights and weekends, run the business from memory, and are "personally the bottleneck". Many have been burned by a cheap VA before, so they want someone vetted, trained and managed who can run with them fast, sound like them, and use AI for them. They buy outcomes (time back, nothing slipping), not tasks.
