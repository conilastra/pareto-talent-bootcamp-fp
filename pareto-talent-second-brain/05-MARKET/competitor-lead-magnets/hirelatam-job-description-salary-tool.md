# HireLATAM — Job Description Generator + LATAM Salary Estimator

## Format
Interactive tool: draft a role, edit responsibilities, get an editable job description plus a directional monthly salary range (example shown: $1,400-$2,200 for an Administrative Assistant).
## Promise
"Build a job description and estimate LATAM salary ranges."
## Who it's for
Companies about to hire in LatAm that don't know the role definition or the cost.
## How they capture
No email asked at any point (confirmed by Conil). It is a live, ungated tool; the CTA after the result is "Have us hire" or "Contact us", i.e. it leads straight to HireLATAM's hiring service.
## How they qualify
Implicit: company name, industry, role, expertise level (entry/mid/expert), time zone, company description, schedule, success definition and goals, tools, must-haves, responsibilities. By the end HireLATAM has a full role brief.
## What happens right after
Result on screen, then a prompt to have HireLATAM hire for the role.
## Strengths
Instant, useful result; effortful inputs self-qualify buyers and double as a role brief; the CTA is the natural next step.
## Weaknesses
Cost-led (salary range); long form; tool for any role, not delegation or founder-specific.
## What Pareto can do better
Keep the instant on-screen result and the self-qualifying inputs, but shorter and about the founder's time and delegation, not the salary. See [[competitive-landscape]].
