## Patterns across competitor lead magnets
| | Athena assessment | BELAY worksheet | South 10X course | DonnaPro quiz | Viva calculators |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Format | 10-question quiz | worksheet + guide | free email course | 5-question quiz, result by team size | 3 calculators |
| Promise | where your delegation gaps are | 5 tasks → 15 h/week | learn to delegate, 50+ tasks | is an EA right for you | hours and dollars an EA gives back |
| Friction | low-medium (name, company email and source at the end) | none on the original page (direct PDF); name + email + optional phone on the hub page | low (email) | none before the call | none (no email) |
| Qualification | none | none in the flows tested | none | team size, role, timeline; commitment on the booking form | none |
| Personal result | named level by email only (not on screen) | no | no | headline by team size | yes, on screen (hours, value) |
| After opt-in | thank-you page (no result) + result email + soft "See how it works" | thank-you page with 4-step process, consultant call | email 1 with 50 ideas, "Start hiring" | booking form for a free strategy session | "Book a Call" |

**The opening for Pareto:** Athena already proves the quiz format works in this market, but it doesn't qualify and doesn't route anyone to a call. BELAY's worksheet is generic and (as tested) gives the PDF directly or behind a short name-and-email form, with no qualification. Nobody combines an **instant on-screen personal result** with **2–3 qualifying questions** and **routing qualified founders straight to a calendar**. Rose has no lead magnet; Boldly has only a newsletter and a checklist. HireLATAM has an interactive JD + salary tool ([[hirelatam-job-description-salary-tool]]), CloudTask an ungated guide library ([[cloudtask-guide-library]]), and South a free email delegation course ([[south-10x-delegation-course]]), the only magnet in Pareto's delegation territory. BELAY also runs vertical versions with an email-only gate ([[belay-financial-advisors-delegation-guide]]). Every competitor magnet is cost-led or generic and none qualifies before the call.
