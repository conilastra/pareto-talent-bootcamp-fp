# Four Cs of communication

**Clear** ("write so that you cannot be misunderstood") · **Complete** (everything needed to act without asking) · **Concise** ("Length is not effort") · **Call to Action** ("Never end open").
Example: "I received this invoice… already paid… duplicate. Should I go ahead and refund him?"
