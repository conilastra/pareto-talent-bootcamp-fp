# Context engineering

"Context is king. Prompt is second." **Gather → Clean → Load → Ask.** Six daily context sources: Fathom calls, YouTube transcripts, Google Drive, website scraping, dictation, email threads. Project = Instructions + Knowledge + Examples; feed at least three source types (transcript, document, website): "One source is just imitation. Several give you a voice." Five instruction lines: who you are, who you write for, how they sound (rules, not adjectives), what you never do, what done looks like.
